It is redundant to say, after it has become clear, that Imam al-Haramayn al-Juwayni (may Allah have mercy on him) is the first to attempt to establish the validity of reasoning by appropriate description through the adaptation of the Sharia for the purpose of examining it in light of its objectives of public interest (maqasid al-sharia) - and Allah knows best - as he divided the rulings according to their goals or objectives or meanings into five categories. The matter later evolved in the hands of the supporters of this methodology to consist of three categories along with their complements, which are the necessities, the needs, and the enhancements, along with the complements of each. He stated under the title: "On the divisions of reasons and principles": "What these individuals have mentioned are the principles of the Sharia (1). We divide them into five categories:
"First: What is rational and is a principle. The rational meaning of it leads to a necessary matter that must be adhered to, and this is akin to the ruling of the Sharia mandating retribution at its proper time, as it is justified by the preservation of the sanctity of blood and the deterrence from aggression against it. When the discerning observer clarifies this in the principle of retribution, he engages with it and extends it to where the essence of this meaning is realized, which is where the justification of its origin becomes easy... It has been established in the Sharia that once the foundations of the principles are established, there is no need to seek the realization of their meanings in individual cases" (2).
He said in this section: "It is what is based on necessity" (3). This section is what later came to be known as the necessities, which refer to the essential universals, namely the preservation of religion, life, intellect, lineage, and wealth.

(1) The term "principles of the Sharia" here refers to the legal rulings, as a legal ruling, if it is justified, is a principle upon which analogy is based, and it is one of the pillars of analogy. Likewise, if it is a subject of research and examination to understand its purpose or its reason.
(2) Al-Juwayni, al-Burhan. 2/602, paragraph: 901.
(3) The same source, p: 604, paragraph: 906.
