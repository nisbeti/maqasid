And perhaps the strong arguments of the followers of the Abu Hanifa school (may Allah be pleased with him) are what led al-Juwayni to further research and delve deeper until he presented the idea of the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) in the form that we will explain, and thus it is presumed that he was the first to articulate it in this manner.

Al-Juwayni discusses the claim that reasoning is a form of adjudicating opinions in the Sharia, saying: "As for what the judge (1) mentioned regarding the matter being beyond control and resorting to the dissolution of returning the matter to the opinions of those with sound minds, this only applies to Malik (may Allah be pleased with him) and his followers if what has been narrated from him is correct" (2).

He said about Malik (may Allah have mercy on him): "His opinion establishes interests that are far removed from the usual interests and known meanings in the Sharia, and this led him to innovate killing and taking wealth based on interests that he presumed to be valid, even if he found no basis for those interests in the foundational texts, and there is no stopping at that; rather, his opinion is whatever his insight leads him to, and it is detached from the suspicions and ulterior motives" (3).

He then said: "We say to Malik (may Allah have mercy on him): Is it permissible to rely on every opinion? If he refuses, we find no reference to which we can adhere except for the approximation that al-Shafi'i (may Allah be pleased with him) accepted, and if he did not mention a standard and stated that what has no text or basis is returned to personal opinion and the approval of those with intellect, then this is now a significant intrusion and a departure from control" (4). He then said: "This is a difficult compound that no devout person would dare to engage in, and its course is to return the matter to the minds of the wise and the judgments of the learned, and we are certain that the matter is contrary to that... If what Malik (may Allah be pleased with him) said—if it is correct—were permissible, the wise during the time of Khosrow Anushirvan would have taken justice and governance into consideration, and this leads to confusion that cannot be independently managed. If Malik (may Allah have mercy on him) and his followers approach the aspect of opinion from the established principles in the Sharia, then what they brought forth is the school of al-Shafi'i (may Allah have mercy on him)" (6).
