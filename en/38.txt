And I ask for success and assistance ... in this matter from our Lord, Exalted is He.


This composition was created in the year 820 AH(1). The author did not mention his name, and therefore attributing it to Abu Yahya is not certain(2).
What draws attention to the lack of care is that this system is still in manuscript form and has not been fully printed.
As for the attention of contemporary scholars to the book (al-Muwafaqat), Abu Ubaidah Mashhoor bin Hassan Al-Salman mentioned in his introduction to his commentary on (al-Muwafaqat) that he came across three abridgments of the book:
The first is: "Al-Marafiq 'ala al-Muwafiq" by Mustafa bin Muhammad Fadl bin Mamin, who passed away in 1328 AH - 1910 CE. It is a poem with a brief explanation, and this poem was printed along with its explanation in 1324 AH in Tunis(3).
The second is: "The Summary of the Muwafaqat" by Ibrahim ibn Tahir ibn As'ad al-Azhim (died 1377 AH - 1957 CE). It is a manuscript in two parts with the author's family(4).
The third is: "Clarifying the Issues in the Summary of the Muwafaqat" by Muhammad Yahya ibn Umar al-Mukhtar ibn al-Talib Abdullah al-Walati al-Shanqiti, who passed away in the year 1330 AH - 1912 CE(5).
