Until his life came to an end ... in the year ninety to seven hundred(1)

Ahmad Baba al-Tanbukti said: "He passed away on Tuesday in the month of Sha'ban in the year 790, and I have not come across his date of birth, may Allah have mercy on him."(2)

As for his birth, there is no verified statement regarding it. The Tanbukti, whose book is considered one of the most precise references for his biography, states that he did not come across the date of his birth.

Dr. Hamadi al-Abidi has favored the view that his birth was around 730 AH based on the fact that he was a close friend and peer of the minister Ibn Zamrak, who was born in 733 AH, and based on his understanding that al-Shatibi was young in 756(3).

As for Professor Muhammad Abu al-Ajfan, he preferred in his introduction to the book Fatwas of al-Shatibi(4) that al-Shatibi was born before the year 720 AH, based on the fact that the death of his earlier teacher Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn al-Zayyat was in the year 728 AH. Therefore, at the very least, al-Shatibi must have been young at that time, and he referred to this matter in his introduction to the book al-Ifadat wa al-Inshadat(5).
