If the resemblance is real, then it is not correct to interpret it. Seeking its interpretation is the work of those who deviate. However, if it is additional, then it should be interpreted. He says: "Applying interpretation to the ambiguous requires detail; it must either be from the truly ambiguous or from the additional. If it is from the additional, then it is necessary if it is determined by evidence, just as the general is clarified by the specific, the absolute by the restricted, and the necessary by the contingent, and similar cases, because their totality is the definitive, which has been previously explained. As for if it is from the truly ambiguous, then its interpretation is not necessary" (1).
He said: "Moreover, the righteous predecessors among the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), the Followers, and those who came after them who followed their example did not address these matters nor spoke about them in a way that necessitates determining an interpretation without evidence, and they are the model and example. To this, the verse indicates this by His saying, Exalted is He: { As for those in whose hearts is deviation, they follow what is ambiguous of it } (2) the verse" (3). If the ambiguous is something that should be interpreted, meaning its meaning should be specified, then the interpreted meaning has three conditions. He says: "When interpretation is applied to the ambiguous, three characteristics must be considered in the interpreted meaning: it must refer to a correct meaning in consideration, generally agreed upon among the differing parties, and the interpreted term must be capable of it" (4).
