The friendship between Ibn Zamrak and al-Shatibi does not prevent one from being twenty years older than the other or more. Furthermore, there is no evidence that al-Shatibi was young in the year 756 AH. This statement is a confusion between his two reports, number 66 and number 67, where he mentioned in one of them the date of the death of his earlier teachers, and in the other that he was young, but the two reports are different and unrelated to each other(1).
His Era:
Al-Shatibi lived in the city of Granada(2) during the time of the Nasrid kings, in the state of Banu Nasr, which at that time did not enjoy stability, primarily due to the Christians' attempts to eradicate Islam in Andalusia. To illustrate the conditions of the era in which al-Shatibi lived and to shed light on the features of this period, it is useful to refer to historical milestones in the lives of Muslims in Andalusia.
The Islamic state in Andalusia was founded by Abd al-Rahman ibn Muawiya ibn Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, nicknamed the Falcon of Quraish. He was known as Abd al-Rahman al-Dakhil because he was the first of the Umayyad princes to enter Andalusia after fleeing and escaping from the Abbasids(3). He entered Cordoba in the year 139 AH, as a conqueror, and then he established the foundations of the state, independent from the caliphate, but he did not call himself caliph.
The Abbasids attempted to reclaim Andalusia in the year 149 AH but failed.
