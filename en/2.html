Indeed, Allah - Exalted is He - has revealed the best of speech, a Book that guides to that which is most upright, guiding thereby those who follow His pleasure to the paths of peace. He has clarified and defined the path of truth and faith, as well as the paths of misguidance and the devil, and He commanded to follow His chosen one and intimate friend, saying: { Say, "This is my path; I invite to Allah with insight, I and whoever follows me." } (1). He prohibited following the devil and warned, saying: { And do not follow the footsteps of the devil. } (2). He commanded to oppose and fight him, saying: { Indeed, the devil is to you a foe, so take him as a foe. } (3). He commanded that He alone be worshipped and that none other be worshipped, for what He has deemed good is good, and what He has deemed evil is evil, and there is no one to overturn His decree. { The judgment is only for Allah. He has commanded that you not worship except Him. } (4), { And it is not for a believing man or a believing woman, when Allah and His Messenger have decreed a matter, that they should have any choice in their matter. } (5). The trust that Allah and His Messenger have testified we must carry is to worship Allah alone, to make people worship Him, and to bear witness to this among the people, conveying it to them and subjecting them to it, { And thus We have made you a just nation that you will be witnesses over the people and the Messenger will be a witness over you. } (6).

However, Allah - Glorified and Exalted - has willed that the situation remains as it is, and He is more eloquent than any speech, for those who understand the meaning of the message, the meaning of the trust, the meaning of the testimony, and the meaning of Allah's commands and prohibitions, and what Allah has said. Exalted is He.
