The discussion of al-Shatibi's methodology in understanding the Sharia is a discussion about his innovations in this regard, and what he has raised and highlighted that distinguishes him from his predecessors among the imams of usul (principles) in the rules and foundations of understanding and deduction.
The sources of legislation are the Qur'an, the Sunnah, consensus (ijma), and analogy (qiyas), and the methodology of understanding the Sharia is how to comprehend what these sources have brought. The rules that are relied upon for this purpose include those that relate to all these sources collectively, as well as those that pertain to each of them individually.
In this chapter, al-Shatibi's methodology in this matter will be clarified in three sections. The first addresses the general rules in this regard, that is, those that pertain to all sources. The second discusses the rules for understanding the Qur'an, while the third addresses the rules for understanding the Sunnah.
As for consensus and analogy, they refer back to the Qur'an and the Sunnah, and therefore al-Shatibi did not examine them separately, but rather alluded to them in scattered references throughout his book. He said: "The book of legal proofs: and the consideration of them in general, and concerning each one of them in detail, which are the Book and the Sunnah, consensus, and analogy" (1). He also said: "The second part: concerning the proofs in detail, which are the Book, the Sunnah, consensus, and opinion. And since the Book and the Sunnah are the foundation for everything else, we have limited our consideration to them" (2).
The rules that al-Shatibi discusses regarding the sources of legislation in general are rules that pertain to the four sources, and those that relate to the Qur'an and the Sunnah are built upon and detailed from them. Therefore, I have titled the first section "al-Shatibi's Methodology in Understanding the Sharia," which is the title of the chapter itself. The second section is titled "al-Shatibi's Methodology in Understanding the Qur'an," and the third section is titled "al-Shatibi's Methodology in Understanding the Sunnah."
