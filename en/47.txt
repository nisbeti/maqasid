The appropriateness of a description to serve as a cause for a ruling or an objective intended by the legislator varies according to the perspectives of the scholars and jurists. What one jurist claims to be appropriate may be opposed by another who argues that it is not appropriate. This is based on the differing understandings, perceptions, and intellects of people. Therefore, there must be a legal criterion to indicate whether the description is appropriate or not.
Claiming the appropriateness of a description without a legal proof of its appropriateness is merely an imagination. Thus, the appropriateness is referred to as "ikhala." Some have described it as an inner imagination, meaning it is a personal internal feeling of the individual, and this is not a legal proof. This will become clear in its appropriate context later.
If it is established after legal consideration that the description is appropriate in reason or imagination but is legally rejected, then it is dismissed and is termed as "nullified." If it is established that it is legally accepted, then it is termed as "considered," meaning it is confirmed to be legally valid.
However, the manner in which the consideration of the description or meaning is established to be appropriate and valid for reasoning, or in other words, to be a legally recognized interest (maslaha) for justifying the ruling, has remained a point of contention among scholars. The disagreement regarding appropriateness as a method of reasoning is, in essence, a disagreement regarding how to consider the description. If the meaning of consideration is that the legal evidence indicates a certain matter, then it is legal based on that, and there is no dispute about this. However, the contested consideration, which is the crux of the disagreement, means that the ruling aligns with a certain interest or an appropriate description, and thus a cause is claimed. Therefore, to understand the idea of reasoning based on interest and the concept of objectives (maqasid) according to al-Shatibi, it is necessary to clarify the meaning of consideration or the meaning of the ruling's alignment with the appropriate description in legal terminology. Especially since al-Shatibi came to mediate between the two parties, affirming the idea of reasoning based on the considered appropriateness after setting conditions that may make it acceptable to those who oppose reasoning based on it. This will be detailed when discussing his methodology.
