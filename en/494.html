The conflict between statement and action has a detailed discussion that is not our focus here; what is intended here is to clarify the significance of statement, action, and acknowledgment according to al-Shatibi.

Consensus (ijma):

It has been clarified previously that consensus according to al-Shatibi is from the Sunnah. The consensus referred to is the consensus of the Companions, and it is considered an explanation of the Qur'an and the Sunnah as it reveals a proof that has not been transmitted. He said: "The Sunnah of the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) is a Sunnah that is acted upon and referred to" (1). He also said: "For they were the first to receive that from the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), and they were the direct recipients of the revelation" (2). Therefore, for the validity of considering consensus in explanation, it must be the consensus of the Companions and must refer back to a general Qur'anic principle. It cannot be said that consensus is an absolute source, as the subject here is not consensus itself, but rather the issue that has been reported to have consensus upon it. This is similar to the Sunnah, which is an absolute source, but in detail, it consists of reports from individuals, and likewise, the transmitted consensus of the Companions is reports from individuals.
