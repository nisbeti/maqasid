Al-Shawkani mentioned an example of the strange (ghareeb) in saying: "Considering the type of description in the type of ruling, such as the reasoning for the limit of drinking being eighty lashes, as it is a presumption of slander due to being a presumption of false accusation, thus it must be established in its place by analogy (qiyas) to seclusion (khalwa), as it is a presumption of illicit sexual intercourse (zina), and therefore it is established in its place." (1)

The scholars of usul (principles of jurisprudence) from the Shafi'is stated that the strange appropriate (munasib ghareeb) is valid for reasoning (ta'lil). Al-Amidi said: "Some have denied it, and their denial is unfounded because it provides a presumption for reasoning." (2) Al-Ghazali said: "As for the strange appropriate, this is in the realm of ijtihad (independent juristic reasoning), and it does not seem far-fetched to me that this may prevail in the presumption of some mujtahids, nor is there a decisive evidence indicating the invalidity of their ijtihad." (3)

As for the Hanafis, they rejected it and dismissed every method of appropriateness (munasaba) altogether. This is the essence of their imams' statements: "As for imagination (khayal)... it is a false matter because it is merely a form of presumption, as imagination and presumption are one, and presumption does not suffice in the truth of anything. It cannot be said that presumption is considered in Sharia for the obligation of acting upon it like the report of a single narrator (khabar al-wahid) and analogy (qiyas), for we say: what is considered is that which has a decisive evidence for its obligation to act upon it, not mere presumption, and no evidence has been established here for its consideration in Sharia, thus it must be disregarded." (4)
