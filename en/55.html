One of the conditions for the ruling of the original (asl) is that it must be a legal ruling (hukm shar'i), because the purpose of analogical reasoning (qiyas) on the original is to ascertain the legal ruling(1), and because the subject matter is legal analogy, not logical or rational analogy. The latter is one of the means of knowledge in rational matters, and it is not a pathway to know the ruling of the Sharia, neither through certainty nor conjecture. What is being sought is the ruling of Allah, Exalted is He, and there is no way to it except through textual evidence.

Another condition is that the evidence for the establishment of the ruling of the original must be a legal evidence.(2)

It is also a condition that there should be no deviation from the norms of analogy, such as the number of units of prayer (rak'ahs), the estimation of the amounts of zakat, and the limits and expiations, as they are not rationally understood. An example of this is the acceptance of the testimony of al-Hakim alone, which is an exception to the rule(3), as well as the allowances for travel and wiping over the socks.(4)

Another condition is that the cause (illah) must be established through one of the evidences that indicate it, which are referred to as the pathways of the cause.(5)

As for the conditions of the cause, we will address them in the second section of this chapter.
