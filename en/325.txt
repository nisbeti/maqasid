Al-Shatibi focuses on establishing that the necessary objectives (maqasid) are those that take into account the obligations of the accountable person (mukallaf), thus countering any objections to his previous opinion regarding the intention of compliance and his understanding of public interest (maslaha) and its justification. Therefore, he introduces an important idea within the context of his research, which justifies his statement that he considers the obligations of the accountable person in matters of necessity. This is that the objectives of the Sharia, even though they pertain to the accountable person by affirming their interests, also, in the process, pertain to affirming the interests of others, whether the accountable person has a share in the interests of others, such as a wife or a son, or others whom he desires to fulfill their purposes, or whether he does not have a share in their interests, as in leases and sales and other transactions. He fulfills his share through housing, while others fulfill their share through ownership of the rent, even if he has no desire or inclination whatsoever for the other to fulfill their share.
