If these are the types of ijtihad (independent juristic reasoning), then the ijtihad that is relied upon and has the desired goal is the ijtihad that clarifies the ruling when it is ambiguous for the obligated individuals. This is a matter of differing opinions and does not occur in definitive matters where there is no room for disagreement. It is the ijtihad that requires an understanding of the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) and the ability to derive rulings from the texts based on this understanding. Thus, the ijtihad that is considered valid in Islamic law is that which is issued by its qualified scholars who possess its conditions first and foremost. It is the ijtihad that aims to clarify the legal ruling in matters that are not definitive, especially those issues that lie in between two differing principles, making it difficult to categorize them under one or the other. This is in contrast to matters that are speculative, where there is no definitive ruling, but one side is evidently favored, while the weaker side is a slight possibility. In any case, one who approaches definitive matters without ijtihad is like one who is circling around a protected area and is close to falling into it. He said: "The ijtihad that occurs in the Sharia is of two types: one is the ijtihad that is considered valid in Islamic law, which is issued by its scholars who are knowledgeable about what is required for ijtihad, and this is what has been previously discussed. The second is the invalid ijtihad, which is issued by someone who is not knowledgeable about what is required for ijtihad, for its essence is merely an opinion based on desire and personal interests, a stumbling in blindness and following whims. Thus, any opinion issued in this manner is undoubtedly not considered valid because it is contrary to the truth that Allah has revealed" (1). As for what is required for this ijtihad, it has been previously clarified as: "Understanding the objectives of the Sharia in their entirety and the ability to derive rulings based on that understanding."
