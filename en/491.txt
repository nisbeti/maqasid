As for the issue of freeing the captive and that a Muslim should not be killed for a disbeliever, he said: "As for freeing the captive, it is derived from His saying, Exalted is He: { And if they seek your help in religion, it is your duty to help them } (1), and this applies to those who have not emigrated if they cannot emigrate except by seeking assistance from others; thus, it is upon others to assist. The captive in this regard is more deserving of assistance, and this falls under the reasoning based on analogy (qiyas). As for the prohibition of killing a Muslim for a disbeliever, the scholars have derived it from the Book, such as His saying: { And Allah will never give the disbelievers a way over the believers } (2), and His saying: { The companions of the Fire and the companions of Paradise are not equal } (3), and this verse is more distant" (4).
The clarification through speech, action, and approval:
This is because the Sunnah clarifies the Qur'an, and the Sunnah consists of the sayings, actions, and approvals of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him). The clarification occurs through these three together and separately. Al-Shatibi said: "The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) was a clarifier through his saying, action, and approval" (5). Each of these three has a specific characteristic in indication or clarification that the others do not possess, and the most eloquent clarification is when the saying of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) aligns with his action, and likewise, when the action of the Messenger aligns with his approval; this indicates the highest level of emulation.
