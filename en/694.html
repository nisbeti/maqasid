The Sharia came with qiyas (analogy) and ta'leel (reasoning), and the original principle in transactions or customs is ta'leel, while in acts of worship it is mere obedience without ta'leel. However, the reason is what has been established through a legal pathway or indicated by legal evidence, and it must meet the conditions of a reason; it is not the mere imaginations of minds or suggestions of intellects, let alone the claims of the distorters.
