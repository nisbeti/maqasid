Until his life came to an end ... in the year 790 (1)

Ahmad Baba al-Tanbukti said: "He passed away on Tuesday in the month of Sha'ban in the year 790, and I have not found any information about his birth, may Allah have mercy on him" (2). As for his birth, there is no verified account. Al-Tanbukti, whose book is considered one of the most accurate references for his biography, states that he has not found the date of his birth. Dr. Hamadi al-Abidi has suggested that his birth was close to 730 AH based on the fact that he was a contemporary of the minister Ibn Zamrak, who was born in 733 AH, and based on his understanding that al-Shatibi was young in 756 (3). As for Professor Muhammad Abu al-Ajfan, he has suggested in his introduction to the book Fatwas of al-Shatibi (4) that al-Shatibi was born before 720 AH based on the fact that the death of his earlier teacher Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn al-Zayyat was in 728 AH. Therefore, at the very least, al-Shatibi must have been youthful at that time, and he referred to this matter in his introduction to the book al-Irfadat wa al-Inshadat (5).
