And the Spaniards began to wreak havoc and destruction in the lands of Andalusia. Despite all this, Granada remained strong, and peace treaties were concluded between the Muslims and the Spaniards, including a peace treaty with Pedro IV in the year 736 - 789 AH, 1336 - 1387 CE. Security and peace prevailed in the realms of the Kingdom of Granada, and it flourished. Despite the Spaniards' attempts to divide the properties of Granada, they failed(1).

In the year 867 AH, Gibraltar fell into the hands of the Castilians, which was a severe blow to the Muslims, and the arrival of supplies from Morocco to Al-Andalus came to a halt(2).

And the rule of Granada was taken over in the year 887 AH - 1482 CE by Muhammad ibn Ali, nicknamed Abu Abdullah al-Saghir, who agreed to surrender Granada in the year 897 AH - 1492 CE to the monarchs of Castile and Aragon, and he departed to Morocco. Thus, the Islamic state in Andalusia came to an end, and it was said about this Abu Abdullah al-Saghir (the light):

Weep like women for a kingdom that was lost... You did not preserve it like men.

The political situation:

This was a reference to historical milestones and events that the Muslims of Andalusia went through. These events explain the migration of the al-Shatibi family to Granada. They also clarify the political situation of the time of al-Shatibi, but there are events that shed greater light on the political situation of the time of al-Shatibi, that is, the eighth century of the Hijra.
