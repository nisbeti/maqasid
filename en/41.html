Chapter One

An Introduction to the Emergence of the Idea of the Objectives of the Sharia

Introduction to the Emergence and Development of the Science of Usul al-Fiqh (principles of jurisprudence):

Imam al-Shafi'i (may Allah have mercy on him) is considered the first to define the principles of deduction and organize them into general and comprehensive rules. Al-Zarkashi said: "Al-Shafi'i (may Allah be pleased with him) was the first to compile a work on the principles of jurisprudence. He authored the book al-Risala, the book of the Rules of the Qur'an, the book of the Differences of Hadith, the book of the Invalidity of Istihsan (preference), the book of the Gathering of Knowledge, and the book of Qiyas" (2).

Sheikh Muhammad Abu Zahra said: "Indeed, al-Shafi'i was worthy of being the first to document the rules of deduction" (3).

Before al-Shafi'i, the jurists would exercise ijtihad (independent reasoning) without having clear boundaries for deduction before them; rather, they relied on their understanding of the meanings of the legal texts, their reasons, and what their texts and objectives implied.
