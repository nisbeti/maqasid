One of the examples of a barrier is a man who has the minimum threshold for zakat but is in debt; the debt prevents him from fulfilling his zakat obligation. If he incurred the debt out of necessity, there is no issue with that. However, if he took on debt to avoid the obligation of zakat, this is an incorrect action. He said: "And if it is the second case, which is that he does it, for example, as a means of preventing the effect of the cause that necessitates that what is required does not occur, then this is an incorrect action." (1)
