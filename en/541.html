The concept of the outcomes of actions (ma'alat al-af'al) is one of the most important ideas in al-Shatibi's methodology, and many rules are built upon it. In fact, considering the outcomes is the primary foundation of his approach. The essence of his idea is that the Sharia was revealed for the benefit of the servants, as the Sharia has outcomes, and the rulings have outcomes. For the sake of these outcomes, the Sharia was established; thus, the Sharia and its rulings are causes for their effects. Likewise, al-Shatibi's assertion that the actions of Allah — Exalted is He — and His rulings are rationally justified is based on the examination of texts whose explicit meanings indicate the purpose behind the action or ruling. A permissible action is permissible for a purpose intended by the legislator, which is the benefit aimed at by the establishment of the ruling. Conversely, an action that is prohibited has an outcome or result that the legislator intended to eliminate, which is the harm intended to be removed by the prohibition.

These benefits and harms are the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia), meaning they are the objectives of the legislator in establishing the Sharia from the outset. This is a fundamental principle in legislation according to al-Shatibi's methodology, where actions are sought to be performed or avoided for their outcomes. Permissible actions may lead to results that are not intended by the legislator, and likewise, prohibited actions may lead to the removal of results that are not intended to be eliminated by the legislator. Therefore, not everything that results from an action is intended by the legislator. The intended outcome, whether it is to be established or removed, is understood through examination, and this has been detailed previously. The outcome that is established through beneficial examination to the point of certainty that it is intended by the legislator, whether in existence or removal, is a foundation for legislation, or a reason for legislation, or a benefit intended by the legislator through demand, or a harm intended to be removed by prohibition. In summary, these are the objectives of the Sharia or the rulings or the outcomes of the Sharia or the rulings.
