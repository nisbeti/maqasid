These actions, texts, and rulings serve as means in relation to the objectives (maqasid), or as causes in relation to the outcomes or effects. It must be noted here that not every effect resulting from a cause is intended by the legislator. The mere fact that something is a result does not indicate that it is an end or an objective. For example, the marriage of a woman who has been divorced three times leads to her being lawful for the first husband if the second husband divorces her, but this lawful status is not an end or objective of the legislator, according to al-Shatibi, as marriage with this intention is prohibited. The end is a result and an effect, but not every result or effect is an end or an objective. This is central to al-Shatibi's ideas, and it will become clear in its place, if Allah wills.

Chapter Three

The Criteria of Objectives According to al-Shatibi

Subject of this chapter:

Al-Shatibi dedicated the second part of his book, al-Muwafaqat, to the subject of objectives, dividing them into two categories: the objectives of the legislator and the objectives of the obligated person. The first part of al-Muwafaqat is titled the Book of Rulings, in which he discusses the legal rulings, the rulings of obligation, and the rulings of status.

The topics he highlighted in the Book of Rulings were previously discussed by the imams of usul (principles) before him, some of which are new. The novelty lies in the perspective on these rulings, whether they are obligatory, recommended, permissible, disliked, or prohibited, or whether they pertain to cause, condition, impediment, determination, concession, validity, corruption, or nullity, viewed in a way that serves the idea of the objectives of the legislator and in a manner that defends against any objection or refutation. Thus, he establishes rules for understanding these rulings and their meanings or objectives. Additionally, before he begins the Book of Rulings, he presents thirteen preliminaries, which he considers necessary for understanding his book.
