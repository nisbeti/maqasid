Bukhari narrated from Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him) that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Actions are judged by intentions, and for every person is what he intended. So whoever's migration is for Allah and His Messenger, then his migration is for Allah and His Messenger; and whoever's migration is for a worldly benefit he may attain or a woman he may marry, then his migration is for that which he migrated towards" (1). And in Muslim, it is narrated in the wording: "Indeed, actions are judged by intentions, and indeed, for every person is what he intended..." (2).

Al-Suyuti transmitted the consensus (tawatur) from the scholars regarding the significance of this hadith (3). Likewise, Al-Hafiz al-Asqalani said: "It has been transmitted in consensus from the scholars regarding the significance of this hadith. Abu Abdullah said: There is nothing in the reports of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) that is more comprehensive, richer, and more beneficial than this hadith" (4). It has been reported from many scholars, including al-Shafi'i and Ahmad ibn Hanbal (may Allah be pleased with them), that it constitutes one-third of Islam. And from al-Shafi'i, that it encompasses seventy chapters of jurisprudence (5).
