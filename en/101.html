It is a suitable description supported by many rulings, or at least two rulings: one ruling that demonstrates the consideration of the specific description in the specific ruling, and another ruling or rulings from which it is inferred that the description has been recognized in the ruling or its genus, or its genus in the genus of the ruling.

However, it should be noted here the difference between the Imams of the Hanafi school and others, all of whom mentioned these types. The Hanafis did not permit the generalization of the description or ruling based solely on the existence of evidence for it in other rulings; rather, they stipulated that the effect of the description or its genus must be established by legal evidence and not merely by the occurrence of the ruling in accordance with it. Just as they required that the essence (ayn) in the essence must be impactful, they also required impact in all types of consideration. And just as they did not see the occurrence of the ruling in accordance with the description as indicative of reasoning, they also did not see any indication of generalization or categorization in the abundance of evidence. Al-Bazdawi said: "Justice for us is the effect" (1), and the commentator in Kashf al-Asrar said: "As for the reference to the principles, it does not lead to modification, and the utmost that can be said is that the texts are in agreement with the description, resulting in an abundance of parallels, but abundance of parallels does not confer strength to the description" (2), and he said: "Because the description is a cause for a legal ruling just as the ruling is, it cannot be established except by legal evidence" (3).

Thus, what is appropriate is what has been established regarding the consideration of the genus of the description in the essence of the ruling or in its genus, or the essence of the description in its genus. The disagreement regarding it is in the establishment of consideration. If the establishment is by evidence, it is agreed upon among the analogists; if it is by the testimony of the ruling to the description and the existence of parallels for it, then there is much disagreement regarding it.
