The intended meaning here is the preservation of its general principles as stated, which is what is meant by His saying, Exalted is He: { This day I have perfected for you your religion } (1) as well; it is not intended to refer to specific issues. If that were the case, no specific detail of the Sharia would be exempt from preservation, and this is not the case, as we assert the permissibility and support it with occurrences due to the differences in conjectures and the possibilities that arise in specific texts, and the occurrence of errors in them is certain. Errors have been found in the reports of the singular narrators and in the meanings of the verses, which indicates that what is meant by the preserved mention is that which is general in nature (2). The comparison between the texts mentioned above indicates the stated difference between the specific and the branch.
