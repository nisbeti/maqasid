One of the responses to this reasoning is that Allah is Wise in His actions and in His refraining from actions; He is Wise absolutely. However, what necessitates that Allah's actions be closer to what is rational must be based on knowledge and reason that only He possesses, which elevates His wisdom to that of Allah's, to the limitless. As for one whose wisdom is nothing compared to Allah's wisdom, what rationality is he speaking of?! Such statements reflect a negligence in comparing Allah to humans, which can be said about the actions of rational beings and wise individuals. Allah - Exalted is He - is Wise, and humans do not comprehend His wisdom because they only grasp some of the wisdom and rationalities that pertain to their tangible experiences, while the unseen world is concealed from people. What necessitates that the effects or manifestations of Allah's wisdom be related to human perceptions and tangible experiences, when Allah - Exalted is He - says: { And Allah knows, while you do not know } (1).
