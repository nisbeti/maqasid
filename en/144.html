Therefore, this idea - the idea of objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) - has not negatively or positively affected the principles of the Hanafi school (madhhab). Al-Ansari said: "The Hanafis do not accept the concept of istihsan (juridical preference) at all, because although it may lead to a presumption, it is not binding on the legislator (Shari) according to what it indicates. It is also not presumed that the legislator has established it for the sake of the many exceptions, as in cases of known cancellation, such as arduous crafts, which are more suitable for alleviation than the hardship of travel. However, the legislator considered the former and disregarded the latter. As for the public interests (masalih mursala), they are also suitable, but they have not been considered in the Sharia. If they do not lead to a presumption of the legislator's consideration, they are not a legal proof but rather a product of the whims of the mind and thus are not considered. Perhaps this is what Nasafi meant, that there is no presumption of the legislator's consideration, which does not contribute anything to the truth. If you say: istihsan certainly leads to presumption and consensus has been established on the consideration of presumption, he said: the consensus on acting upon presumption is only on the condition that it is - that is, the presumption - legally established from the direction of the Sharia and a presumption according to the legislator" (1).

As for what the proponents of the argument have said regarding the idea of maqasid, that the meaning has led to many rulings in accordance with it, it is stronger than analogy (qiyas) and serves as a basis for legislation. They have referred to the testimony of the rulings as being in accordance with the principles or conforming to the principles, but the Hanafis have also rejected this. The commentator on the principles of al-Bazdawi said: "As for the texts being in accordance with the description, it results in a multitude of similarities, and with the multitude of similarities, strength in the description is not achieved" (2).
