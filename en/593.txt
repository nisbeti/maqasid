And the discussion on the conflict between the original ruling and the predominant ruling will come within the discussion of the controls of the principle of blocking the means (sadd al-dhara'i).
Controls for applying the principle of blocking the means:
The place for applying the principle is in the permissible (mubah), as has been clarified. It is in the permissible that leads to something forbidden (haram). However, this is not applicable to any permissible act, nor to any manner of reaching the forbidden, nor to any forbidden act. Therefore, it is necessary to specify the controls and restrictions for applying this principle.
The first control:
That the permissible act is not permissible due to necessity or need. For what is such, opposing it with the forbidden outcome or with any objections it may encounter from evils is not valid. This is because the assumption is that the obligated person is compelled to it, and necessities are generally excused in the Sharia. In fact, this permissible act may have become obligatory, such as consuming carrion in a state of starvation, or it is needed to the extent that if it is abandoned, hardship will ensue. The Sharia has indicated the removal of hardship, and prohibiting an action due to its outcome leads to hardship for the obligated person. This indicates that the permissible action taken out of necessity or need is not a place for applying the principle of blocking the means.
