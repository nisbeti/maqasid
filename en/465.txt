From these two aspects of adherence and commitment, knowledge of the Qur'an, its methods, and meanings increases, leading to the benefit of understanding its meanings and a thorough exploration of them, which results in the comprehension of the overarching principles of the Qur'an. Al-Shatibi established two principles or foundations, perhaps intending to provide examples of what can be gained from engaging with the Qur'an and exploring its meanings. The first benefits the extraction of meanings by referring to a general principle. The second pertains to the conduct and etiquette of the Qur'an.
As for the first, it is: "Every narrative that occurs in the Qur'an must either have a response before it or after it, which is more common, or not. If there is a response, there is no doubt about the invalidity or falsehood of that narrative. If there is no response accompanying it, that is evidence of the validity and truth of that narrative."
