As for the third, it is the consideration of the conditions of the accountable individuals (mukallafīn) in particular. This relates to understanding the objectives (maqasid) but with a finer focus. The jurist (mujtahid) issues rulings on the accountable individuals according to their specific circumstances, relying on what Allah has granted him of wisdom and the light He has bestowed upon him. This relates to the realization of the legal rule (taḥqīq al-manāṭ) - according to al-Shatibi - but it is a specific type of it. He said: "The realization of the legal rule is of two types: a general realization, which has been mentioned, and a specific realization from that general one." As for the first, which is the general, it has been clarified previously. As for the second, he said: "The second, which is the specific consideration, is higher and more precise than this and is, in reality, derived from the result of piety mentioned in His saying, Exalted is He: { If you are conscious of Allah, He will grant you a criterion. } (3), and it may be referred to as wisdom, as indicated by His saying, Exalted is He: { He gives wisdom to whom He wills, and whoever is given wisdom has certainly been given much good. } (4)." He said: "The one who possesses this specific realization is the one who has been granted light by which he recognizes souls, their intentions, their varying levels of understanding, and their capacity to bear obligations and their patience in enduring their burdens or their weakness. He knows whether they are inclined towards immediate gains or not, so he applies to each soul from the rulings of the texts what is appropriate for it, based on the understanding that this is the legal objective in receiving obligations. It is as if he specifically addresses the generality of the accountable individuals and the obligations with this realization." Then he said: "This is the meaning of the realization of the legal rule here." Al-Shatibi supported this type with examples from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him).
