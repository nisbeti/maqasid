The third is the consideration of the conditions of the accountable individuals in particular. This relates to understanding the objectives (maqasid) but with a finer focus. The jurist (mujtahid) issues rulings on the accountable individuals based on their specific circumstances, relying on what Allah has granted him of wisdom and the light He has bestowed upon him. This is related to the realization of the legal ruling (tahqiq al-manat) - according to al-Shatibi - but it is a specific type of it. He said: "The realization of the legal ruling is of two types: a general realization, which has been mentioned(1), and a specific realization from that general one"(2). As for the first, which is general, it has been clarified previously. As for the second, he said: "The second, which is the specific consideration, is higher and more precise than this and is, in fact, derived from the result of piety mentioned in His saying, Exalted is He: { If you fear Allah, He will grant you a criterion }(3). It may also be referred to as wisdom, as indicated in His saying, Exalted is He: { He gives wisdom to whom He wills, and whoever is given wisdom has certainly been given much good }(4)"(5). He said: "The possessor of this specific realization is the one who has been granted light by which he understands the souls and their intentions, the differences in their perceptions, their capacity to bear obligations, and their patience in enduring their burdens or their weakness. He knows their inclination towards immediate gains or their lack of inclination, so he applies to each soul from the rulings of the texts what is appropriate for it, based on the understanding that this is the legal objective in receiving obligations. It is as if he specifies the generality of the accountable individuals and the obligations with this realization"(6). He then said: "This is the meaning of the realization of the legal ruling here"(7). Al-Shatibi supported this type with examples from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him).
