The follower (muqallid) may find someone to clarify the ruling for him, or he may not find anyone. If he does not find anyone, he is relieved of the obligation according to al-Shatibi. If he does find someone, the opinions regarding his issue may either be unified or varied. If they are unified, there is no problem; but if they are varied, he must prefer one over the others, and he must have a legal basis for his preference; it is not permissible for him to choose arbitrarily. Al-Shatibi said: "There is no difference between the case of the mujtahid encountering evidence and the case of the layman encountering a mufti. The conflict between the two fatwas is upon him just as the conflict between two pieces of evidence is upon the mujtahid. Just as it is not permissible for the mujtahid to follow both pieces of evidence together or to follow one of them without exerting effort (ijtihad) or preference, it is likewise not permissible for the layman to follow both muftis together or to follow one of them without exerting effort or preference. The statement of those who said that if they conflict he may choose (1) is incorrect." (2)
