"Then Sultan Muhammad V, known as al-Ghani billah, assumed the emirate and continued from 755 AH to 793 AH. His rule was subjected to bloody events during this period. He faced a coup in the year 760, after which he fled to Morocco and returned in 763. Granada experienced three successive coups during these three years: the first in the month of Ramadan in the year 760, which ended with the flight of al-Ghani billah to Morocco and the ascension of his brother Ismail II in his place. The second coup occurred in Sha'ban in the year 761, in which Ismail II was killed, and the killer, Abu Said al-Barmikhu, married his sister and took the throne. The third coup took place in Jumada al-Thani in the year 763, which resulted in the return of al-Ghani billah to his throne, and al-Barmikhu was killed by the King of Castile."

"Some sources indicate that al-Barmikhu was addicted to hashish, and this vice spread among the people openly, and poems were composed about it."
