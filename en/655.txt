The second: The general ruling that extends from the inferred (mustaqra) to what has not been inferred does not extend to what does not contain the cause (illah). Thus, the ruling on iron does not extend to anything that is not like it. Likewise for wood, for humans, and for the sun and the moon; one cannot rule on the moon tomorrow in the same way as one rules on the sun tomorrow, nor vice versa, because the cause in both cases, although it is the system, is different: the system of the sun is not the same as the system of the moon. The reason for the generality is the cause, not the inference. This is the incomplete inference in material phenomena; it is only beneficial if one knows that a cause exists. How then can it be applied to the texts of the legislator, which have multiple meanings? If inference requires a cause to be beneficial, and al-Shatibi uses inference to understand the causes, then each of the two needs the other and does not exist except after it. Therefore, the method of incomplete inference is invalid and rejected, and it does not exist at all in the legal texts. These causes will not be found by this method; even if they are found, they will be discovered through deduction as legal causes, not material or logical causes.
The opposition of the particular to the general: Al-Shatibi held that the general does not break down due to the opposition of particulars to it, based on the fact that the general has been established completely through inference, thus it is definitive, while the particular is speculative. Either the particular must be interpreted to agree with the general, or it must be rejected. He also maintained that it is necessary to employ both the generals and the particulars together; one cannot do without the generals in favor of the particulars, nor without the particulars in favor of the generals. These two matters are correct in terms of their outcomes, but they do not align based on his methodology, meaning they do not align based on the fact that the generals are inferential generals derived from the particulars. In this matter, there is a contradiction that al-Shatibi attempted to resolve.
