Al-Shatibi holds that the principle of blocking the means (sadd al-dhara'i) is definitive through induction and is applied. It is indicated by the meanings of the rulings derived from the texts, such as His saying, Exalted is He: {And do not insult those they invoke besides Allah, lest they insult Allah in enmity without knowledge.} (1). And His saying, Exalted is He: {O you who have believed, do not say [to the Messenger], "Consider us," but say, "Look at us," and listen.} (2). And the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said in the authentic narration: "Indeed, among the greatest sins is cursing a man's parents." They said, "O Messenger of Allah, can a man curse his parents?" He said, "Yes, he insults the father of a man, and thus he insults his father, and he insults his mother, and thus he insults his mother." (3). And he (peace be upon him) refrained from killing the hypocrites because it would lead to the hypocrites saying, "Muhammad is killing his companions." (4) (5).

(1) Surah Al-An'am, 108.

(2) Surah Al-Baqarah, 104.

(3) Bukhari (5516) with the wording "curses" instead of "curses," and Muslim (130) with the same wording, and Tirmidhi (1824), who said: Hasan Sahih, and Ahmad (6243).

(4) Bukhari (3527) and (4525), and Muslim (4682) and (1761), which mention the hypocrites and the Khawarij, and Tirmidhi (3237) who said: Hasan Sahih, and Ahmad (14292).

(5) For a review of the evidence for the principle according to Al-Shatibi, see Al-Muwafaqat: 1/72, 2/250, 3/126, 127.
