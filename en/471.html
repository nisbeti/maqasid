Al-Shatibi affirms his statement by noting that one of the first surahs revealed in Mecca was Surah Al-An'am, and one of the first revealed in Medina was Surah Al-Baqarah. He says: "For indeed it (1) was revealed clarifying the foundations of beliefs and the principles of religion, and from it the scholars derived the principles of monotheism (tawhid) that the theologians classified, from the proof of the necessary existence to the proof of the Imamate. This is what they said" (2). He then stated: "And when the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) migrated to Medina, one of the first things revealed to him was Surah Al-Baqarah, which established the principles of piety based on the principles of Surah Al-An'am" (3). After mentioning some details regarding this, he said: "Other later Medina surahs are based on it, just as other later Meccan surahs are based on Al-An'am. And if you were to compare the other surahs with one another in order, you would find them similarly aligned, so let not the observer of the book overlook this meaning, for it is one of the secrets of the sciences of exegesis (tafsir), and according to one's knowledge of it, one gains knowledge of the words of his Lord, Exalted is He" (4).
