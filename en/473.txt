These three categories fall under one category, which is the intended purpose expressed in His saying, { And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me }(1). The Third Topic
The Methodology of al-Shatibi in Understanding the Sunnah


The subject of this research:
The Sunnah is the second source of legislation after the Book, and it is an explanation of the Book. Al-Shatibi's opinion, as previously mentioned, is that there is no legislation in the Sunnah that is additional to the Book, meaning no legislation that does not have a basis in the Book. The purpose of this discussion is to clarify how the Sunnah explains the Book according to al-Shatibi, and how the established legal rulings in the Sunnah are traced back to their origins in the Book, especially those that may be used to object to his view that the Sunnah is independent in its legislation and has no basis in the Book.
And what is also meant is to clarify his position that what reaches us from the Sunnah (tradition) that cannot be traced back to a source in the Book (Qur'an) must be paused upon, and it is not correct to act upon it. Likewise, this discussion aims to clarify al-Shatibi's view on the indication of actions regarding legal rulings, as the actions of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) are part of the Sunnah, and the indication of actions is not like the indication of words.
Definition of the Sunnah according to al-Shatibi:
Al-Shatibi uses the term "sunnah" as a source of legislation to refer to what has been transmitted from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) in terms of speech, action, or approval, as well as what has been reported from the Companions or the Rightly Guided Caliphs.
