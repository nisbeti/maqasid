The third: Knowing the outcome of the servant so that he fears Allah through it and hopes for Him. These three categories fall under one overarching category, which is indicated by His saying, Exalted is He: { And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me } (1). Thus, worship is the primary objective. (2)

Section Three
The Methodology of al-Shatibi in Understanding the Sunnah

The topic of this section:
The Sunnah is the second source of legislation after the Book, and it serves as an explanation of the Book. Al-Shatibi's opinion has been clarified previously that there is no legislation in the Sunnah that is additional to the Book, meaning legislation that has no basis in the Book. The purpose of this section is to clarify how the Sunnah explains the Book according to al-Shatibi, and how the established legal rulings in the Sunnah are traced back to their origins in the Book, especially those that may be objected to by claiming that the Sunnah has independent legislation with no basis in the Book. It is also intended to clarify his position that what reaches us from the Sunnah, which cannot be traced back to a basis in the Book, must be suspended and cannot be acted upon. Additionally, this section aims to present al-Shatibi's view on the implication of actions regarding legal rulings, as the actions of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) are part of the Sunnah, and the implication of actions is not the same as that of statements.
Definition of the Sunnah according to al-Shatibi:
Al-Shatibi uses the term "Sunnah" as a source of legislation to refer to what has been transmitted from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) in terms of sayings, actions, or approvals, as well as what has been reported from the Companions or the Rightly Guided Caliphs.
