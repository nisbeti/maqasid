He then said: "Contradiction only occurs if the preferred (rajih) is considered less preferred (marjuh) from the perspective of one observer; rather, it is from two observers, each of whom believes that the reason upon which the ruling is based exists in the subject according to what is in the reality as he perceives it and in his assumption." These texts and opinions in al-Shatibi's view emphasize the difference in his understanding of benefit (maslaha) and harm (mafsada) compared to the prevailing understanding among others. He also affirms that the intended meaning for him is that the benefit is what has been established as intended by the Legislator (Shari) regardless of whether it is suitable or unsuitable.

Section Three
The Intention of the Legislator in Establishing the Sharia for Understanding

The meaning of this type:
Al-Shatibi discusses under this type of the objectives of the Legislator five issues related to the Arabic language, in which he establishes the principles that must be followed in order to understand the Sharia from its sources, that is, from the legal texts. He clarifies how meanings are derived from words and structures, what meanings are considered, and what meanings are not considered.
He also discusses whether the meanings of rulings are among the indications of the texts or not. The meanings of rulings are the derived meanings that are taken from the legal objectives.
