As for the definition of the Qur'an regarding rulings being general and not specific, it has been previously explained that the Noble Qur'an contains the explanation of everything; it is comprehensive despite its brevity, and it can only be so if it contains general principles that apply to many specific cases, even if they are not explicitly mentioned. This does not mean that specific cases are not mentioned (1). Therefore, it was said: "Most of it is general." However, these specific cases mentioned in the Qur'an must be definitive or refer back to definitive origins, and they can only be definitive if many specific cases support their meaning, which leads to them being general. This is in contrast to the claim that they are specific; thus, they are specific and must refer back to general principles. Hence, it was said: "Its basis is on the general," just as the prohibition of alcohol is related to the preservation of the intellect, and the prohibition of the worship of idols or specific idols like al-Lat and al-Uzza is related to the worship of Allah alone. This is the meaning of the statement: "Its basis is on the general in the sense of the origin." As for the statement: "Its basis is on the general with consideration," it is due to what may be questioned regarding interpretations or inferences, as will become clear.
What is meant by consideration is that the meaning taken or interpreted must align with the correct meaning that is consistent with the apparent conditions and refers back to an origin, meaning it must be in agreement with it but not identical to it, which is the meaning of consideration as clarified in the first chapter. Therefore, the issue arises from two aspects. The first: the apparent text, as the interpretation may come in a manner not customary in the speech and styles of the Arabs, yet al-Shatibi accepts it. The second aspect of the issue is the internal aspect, such that the meaning may not refer back to a definitive origin and lacks evidence by consideration. Although al-Shatibi's methodology rejects it or suspends judgment on it, he stated that its acceptance is possible in cases where they exist, though they are few.
