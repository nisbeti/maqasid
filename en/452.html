Therefore, the interpretation of the Qur'an and understanding the meanings of its texts is through understanding the inner meaning as mentioned, and not by merely stopping at the apparent meanings of the texts, because understanding the apparent is easy. He said: "For Allah, Exalted is He, said: { So what is [the matter] with those people that they hardly understand a statement? } (1), and the meaning is that they do not understand what Allah intends from the speech, and it is not intended that they do not understand the words themselves, how could they when it was revealed in their language, but they did not attain the understanding of Allah's intent from the speech" (2). Al-Shatibi clarifies his meaning of the inner (batin) with examples, including Ibn Abbas's interpretation of Allah's saying: { When comes the help of Allah and victory } (3) by saying: It is merely the end of the life of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), which he was informed of. And the saying of Umar (may Allah be pleased with him): By Allah, I do not know anything from it except what I have learned (4). Al-Shatibi said: "The apparent meaning of this surah is that Allah commanded His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) to glorify and praise his Lord and seek His forgiveness when Allah granted him victory and opened for him, and its inner meaning is that Allah announced to him his own death" (5). He said: "And when the verse was revealed: { This day I have perfected for you your religion } (6), the companions rejoiced and Umar cried and said: There is nothing after perfection except deficiency, feeling the announcement of his (peace and blessings be upon him) death, and he lived thereafter only eighty-one days" (7).
