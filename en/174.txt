Likewise, the principle of istihsan (preference)(1) according to the opinion of Malik is based on this principle"(2). We observe in this text that the general principle (ma'nawi) is stronger than the text in its indication, because the general principle is definitive in its meaning, whereas the definitiveness of the text in its meaning is difficult or rare if it exists.
The primary and secondary objectives:
Al-Shatibi says in his seventh introduction: "Every religious knowledge is sought by the legislator only as a means to worship Allah, Exalted is He, and not for any other reason. If another consideration appears in it, it is secondary and by way of following, not by way of the primary intention"(3). This is because when a command or prohibition comes from the legislator, the legislator's intention is to establish what is commanded and to avoid what is prohibited, and this is the primary intention. In other words, the primary intention is obedience without regard to the outcomes or benefits, and thus religious knowledge is what aids in knowing Allah's commands and prohibitions, in order to understand how to obey and how to worship, and this is the meaning of his statement: "a means to worship."
