Al-Shatibi says: "The Arabic language, in terms of its words that indicate meanings, has two perspectives: one from the standpoint of being words and expressions that are absolute indicators of absolute meanings, which is the original indication, and the second from the standpoint of being words and expressions that are restricted indicators of serving meanings, which is the secondary indication" (1). He says: "As for the aspect of the original meaning, there is no doubt about its validity in indicating rulings absolutely, and there can be no disagreement in this regard, such as the forms of commands and prohibitions and generalities and particularities... As for the aspect of the secondary meaning, is it valid to consider it in indicating rulings in terms of understanding additional meanings beyond the original meaning or not?" (2).
