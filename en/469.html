The origin itself also requires an examination that leads to certainty about it. He says: "The validity of the mentioned origin is clarified in the book of ijtihad, which is the origin of embodying the attributes of Allah and emulating His actions. It includes various types of foundational principles, subsidiary benefits, and literary merits. Let us mention examples that can assist in understanding the intended meaning. Among these is that non-punishment before warning indicates this, as He, Exalted is He, informed about Himself by saying: { And We did not punish until We sent a messenger } (1). It has become His custom with His creation that He does not hold them accountable for disobedience except after sending the messengers. Once the proof has been established against them, { So whoever wills, let him believe; and whoever wills, let him disbelieve } (2), and for each is a recompense like it" (3). Among these is also the thoroughness in establishing the proof, and the fact that one is not punished for a sin at the first instance, and the improvement of expression through euphemism and similar means in situations where it is necessary to mention what is typically considered shameful in our customs, such as His saying: { Or you have touched women } (4), and { And Mary, the daughter of Imran, who guarded her chastity, and We breathed into her } (5), and others (6).
