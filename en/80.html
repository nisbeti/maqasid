They agreed that the cause (illa) must be indicated by the Sharia. This is to be understood through one of the paths of reasoning (ta'leel). This matter has been clarified in what has preceded.

2- The reason (illah) must mean a motivating factor and not merely an indicator. Al-Amidi said: "They differed on the permissibility of the reason in the original sense being merely an indicator, and the preferred view is that the reason in the original sense must mean a motivating factor, that is, it must include a valid wisdom intended by the Legislator in enacting the ruling. Otherwise, if it were merely a descriptive characteristic without any wisdom, but merely an indicator, then reasoning based on it in the original sense is impossible"(1). This matter has been clarified previously in the explanation of the meanings of the reason.

3- The reason (illa) must be effective in the ruling. Al-Zarkashi said: "In mentioning the conditions of the reason: the first is that it must be effective in the ruling; if it does not affect it, it cannot be considered a reason... Thus, al-Mawardi and al-Ruyani mentioned it, and what they mean by effectiveness is suitability. Better than their expression is the saying of Professor Abu Mansur: that its description must be one that is valid for linking the ruling to it"(2).

He also said: "Some have permitted reasoning based solely on the indicative evidence (al-amara al-tardiyya). The truth is the opposite, and it must include a ruling that motivates the obligated person to comply and serves as a valid witness for linking the ruling to it."(3)

He said: "The judge (al-Qadi) stated in (al-Taqrib): the meaning of the cause (illa) being effective in the ruling is that it is judged that the jurist's (mujtahid) presumption is that the ruling is present when it is established due to it and not due to anything else"(4). There is an important disagreement regarding the condition of effectiveness and its meaning between the Shafi'is and the Hanafis, which I will mention in its place, if Allah wills.
