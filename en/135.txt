The strong arguments of the followers of Abu Hanifa (may Allah be pleased with him) may have led al-Juwayni to further research and delve deeper until he presented the idea of the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) in the form that we will explain, and thus it is presumed that he was the first to articulate it in this manner.
Al-Juwayni discusses the claim that reasoning is a form of adjudicating opinion in the Sharia, saying: "As for what the judge(1) mentioned regarding the matter being beyond regulation and resorting to the dissolution and returning the matter to the opinions of those with sound minds, this only applies to Malik (may Allah be pleased with him) and his followers if what has been narrated about him is correct"(2).
He said about Malik (may Allah have mercy on him): "His opinion establishes benefits that are distant from the usual benefits and the known meanings in the Sharia, and this led him to innovate killing and taking wealth based on benefits that are likely to be required, even if he did not find a basis for those benefits in the principles, and there is no stopping at that; rather, the opinion is his, whatever he sees fit, and it is detached from the suspicions and interests"(3).
He then said: "We say to Malik (may Allah have mercy on him): Is it permissible to rely on every opinion? If he refuses, we find no reference to settle upon except for the approximation that al-Shafi'i (may Allah be pleased with him) accepted, and if he did not mention regulation and stated that what has no text or basis is returned to unregulated opinion and the approval of those with intellect, this is indeed a great intrusion and a departure from regulation"(4). Then he said: "This is a difficult position that no believer would dare to take, and its course is to return the matter to the minds of the wise and the judgments of the judges, and we are certain that the matter is contrary to that... If what Malik (may Allah be pleased with him) said—if it is correct—were permissible, the wise during the time of Khosrow Anushirvan would have taken justice and governance(5) as their criterion, and this leads to chaos that cannot be independently managed. If Malik (may Allah have mercy on him) and his followers approach the opinion from the established principles in the Sharia, then what they have brought forth is the doctrine of al-Shafi'i (may Allah have mercy on him)"(6).
