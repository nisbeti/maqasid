It remains only that the public interest (maslaha) should return to Allah, Exalted is He, and this is invalid, as it involves employing reason in matters where it should not be applied, and taking ignorance as evidence for knowledge. If there must be a statement regarding the public interest and its return to some source, the most correct assertion is that the public interest either returns to Allah, Exalted is He, or to the servant, or to what we do not know, and thus this argument is invalidated.
Some have argued that His actions - Exalted is He - are not subject to reasoning, yet the principle regarding actions is that they are justified by public interest (1), based on the observation of the correlation between rulings and interests. This reasoning is based on incomplete induction, and therefore it is not valid, as it has been established previously that incomplete induction cannot serve as evidence; rather, the evidence is the accompanying cause.
Among the proofs that His actions - Exalted is He - are justified, and that He does not act except for a purpose or intention, is what al-Shatibi has posited, which is based on induction, but it differs from the induction mentioned above - that of al-Razi. Al-Razi conducted an induction of rulings to establish the principle of justification by interests, while denying the justification of actions. As for al-Shatibi, he conducted an induction of texts, by extensively citing the texts that mention intention, as in His saying: { so that He may test you }, { that you may be grateful }, { to worship Me }, { so that you may attain piety }, { so that there will be no argument for the people against Allah after the messengers }. And so on (2).
