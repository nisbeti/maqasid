As for what has come from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), it is clear and agreed upon that it is the Sunnah. As for what has come from the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), it refers to their consensus as a source of legislation based on legal evidence that they recognized but has not been transmitted to us. The actions of the rightly guided caliphs also refer back to the reality of consensus. He said, after mentioning meanings that are referred to as Sunnah: "When the preceding is gathered, it results in four aspects in the general sense: his (peace and blessings be upon him) saying, his action, and his approval, all of which are either received by revelation or by ijtihad (independent reasoning) based on the validity of ijtihad in his case; these are three, and the fourth is what has come from the Companions or the caliphs" (1). He said: "The term Sunnah is also used for what the Companions acted upon, whether it is found in the Book or the Sunnah or not, as it is following a Sunnah that was established among them but has not been transmitted to us, or a consensus reached among them or their caliphs. For their consensus is consensus, and the actions of their caliphs also refer back to the reality of consensus as required by their considerations of public interest (maslaha), which includes under this term the public interests (masalih mursala) and preference (istihsan), as they did in the case of the punishment for wine, the liability of craftsmen, the compilation of the Qur'an, and compelling people to read from one of the seven readings, and the recording of records, and similar matters" (2). He means by consensus the consensus of the Companions, not those after them. He said: "The Sunnah of the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) is a Sunnah that is acted upon and referred to, and evidence for this includes several matters, one of which is Allah's praise of them without exception and His commendation of their justice and what pertains to it" (3). He said: "It cannot be said that this is general for the Ummah and does not specifically pertain to the Companions over those after them, for we say: First, this is not the case based on the fact that they are the ones specifically addressed, and those after them do not enter with them except by analogy and another proof" (4). His intention is that they are the ones specifically addressed, meaning in praise and in commendation and what pertains to that.
