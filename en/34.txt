Al-Shatibi was one of the distinguished scholars of his time, known for his rejection of fanaticism, rigidity, and blind imitation. He was also steadfast in his opinions, intelligent, pious, and knowledgeable, which indicates the independence of his character and the depth of his thought. This earned him the animosity of the rigid and provoked the traditionalists of his era.
The reputation of al-Shatibi spread during his lifetime and transcended the borders of Andalusia. His books indicate the breadth of his knowledge in every branch of the sciences, whether in philosophy and the principles of religion, or in logic and the principles of jurisprudence, or in the sciences of language and the various branches of Islamic law. He himself pointed to this when he said: "Since the time my mind was opened to understanding, and I directed my pursuit towards knowledge, I have examined its intellectual and legal aspects, its principles and branches. I did not limit myself to one type of knowledge over another, nor did I single out one category from among its types... Rather, I plunged into its depths like a skilled swimmer, and I advanced in its fields with the boldness of the courageous... Being oblivious to the words of the speaker and the reproach of the reproacher, and turning away from the rejection of the rejecter and the blame of the blamer until the Most Generous, Most Compassionate Lord bestowed upon me an understanding of the meanings of the Sharia that I had not anticipated... From there, my soul was strengthened to walk in His path as much as Allah made easy for me, so I began with the principles of religion in action and belief, then with its branches built upon those principles" (1).
