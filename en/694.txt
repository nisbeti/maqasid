The Sharia came with qiyas (analogy) and ta'leel (reasoning), and the original principle in transactions or customs is ta'leel, while in acts of worship it is mere obedience without ta'leel. However, the reason is what has been established through a legal pathway or indicated by legal evidence, and it must meet the conditions of a reason; it is not the mere imaginations of minds or suggestions of intellects, let alone the claims of the distorters.

At the conclusion of this epilogue, I ask Allah, the Exalted, for mercy and abundant reward for al-Shatibi, and I ask Him to make this work sincere for His noble face and to benefit others through it. I also ask Him for forgiveness and pardon for anything that my pen may have strayed into, or my tongue may have slipped, or my thoughts and imagination may have exceeded. I ask Him for great reward and recompense for everyone who taught me, advised me, and benefited me in this knowledge and in this book, and to forgive me, my parents, and the believers.
