The Sharia came with analogical reasoning (qiyas) and reasoning (ta'leel), and the basis in transactions or customs is reasoning, while in acts of worship it is devotion and lack of reasoning. However, the reason is what has been established in a legal context or indicated by legal evidence, and it must meet the conditions of reasoning; it is not the figments of the imagination or the proposals of minds, let alone the claims of the distorters.
In conclusion, I ask Allah, the Exalted, for mercy and abundant reward for al-Shatibi, and I ask Him to make this work of mine sincere for His noble face and to benefit others through it. I also ask Him for forgiveness and pardon for anything my pen may have strayed into, or my tongue may have slipped on, or my thoughts and imagination may have exceeded. Furthermore, I ask Him for abundant reward and recompense for everyone who taught me, advised me, and benefited me in this knowledge and in this book, and to forgive me, my parents, and the believers.
