Among the evidence for the fact that tranquility of the heart is an increase in faith is the statement of Ibrahim (peace be upon him): { My Lord, show me how You give life to the dead } (1), and when he was asked: { He said, "Did you not believe?" He said, "Yes, but so that my heart may be at ease." } (2). If Allah, Exalted is He, had known that he had doubt, He would have made it evident, thus it is confirmed that tranquility was in the sense of an increase in faith. This is in contrast to what Allah mentioned about a group of the Bedouins in His saying: { The Bedouins say, "We have believed." } (3), for Allah, Exalted is He, responded to them: { Say, "You have not believed; rather, say, 'We have submitted,' for faith has not yet entered your hearts." } (4) (5).
