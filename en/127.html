The heated debate between those who accept and those who reject the approach of suitability and reasoning has led to the emergence of the concept of the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) and its clarification. I will summarize below the evidence of those who advocate for it, then I will substantiate it with texts from the imams and subsequently clarify the responses of the refuters to show how the idea of the objectives emerged. Below are their arguments in summary:

1 - They stated that the texts and their meanings are insufficient for issues and events. And that no event is devoid of a ruling from Allah, Exalted is He, attributed to the Sharia of Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him). Therefore, it is necessary to provide reasoning for the rulings, and thus the principle in rulings is reasoning. This necessitates reasoning based on what is appropriate, and it requires a disciplined continuation in pursuing meanings. What does not have its reason established through the pathways of text, consensus, indication, and allusion, if an appropriate meaning appears in it, then it must be reasoned with unless it is abrogated or opposed.

2 - They said that the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) would elaborate on understanding the meanings of public interest (maslaha), and those who came after them followed this practice, without establishing criteria for what should be followed and what should not be followed from these meanings. This indicates the validity of this elaboration, provided that these meanings and interests are derived from the sources of the Sharia. The evidence is established in the actions of the Companions.

3 - They said that the appropriate descriptions and meanings, and those similar to them, are matters that relate to conjecture regarding their causes. The legal rulings are derived from conjecture, so the reasoning based on appropriateness is thus legal, as is the evidence. Below are the texts that indicate this:

Al-Juwayni said: "As for al-Shafi'i, he said: 'We know with certainty that no incident is devoid of the ruling of Allah, Exalted is He.'"(1) And he said: "The previous imams did not leave any incident, despite the multitude of issues and the abundance of judgments and fatwas, without the ruling of Allah, Exalted is He... Based on this, we know that they - may Allah be pleased with them - proceeded in establishing rulings with confidence in their applicability to incidents, actively seeking to affirm them in what arises and occurs."(2)

(1) Al-Juwayni, Al-Burhan. 2/723, paragraph: 1133.

(2) Al-Juwayni, al-Burhan. 2/723, paragraph: 1133.
