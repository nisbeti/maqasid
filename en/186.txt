Al-Shatibi said in his first introduction: "The preservation guaranteed in His saying, Exalted is He: { Indeed, We sent down the Reminder, and indeed, We will preserve it } (1) refers to the preservation of its general principles that are explicitly stated, which is also what is meant by His saying, Exalted is He: { This day I have perfected for you your religion } (2). It does not refer to specific issues, for if that were the case, no specific aspect of the Sharia would be neglected in preservation, and this is not the case, as we can assert the permissibility, supported by occurrence, due to the variation of conjectures and the possibility of errors in specific texts, and the occurrence of mistakes in them is certain. Mistakes have been found in the reports of the singular narrators and in the meanings of the verses, which indicates that what is meant by the preserved Reminder is that which is general" (3).
He said in his ninth introduction: "What is definitive or related to a definitive origin, and the blessed Sharia of Muhammad was revealed in this manner, and therefore it is preserved in its roots and branches, as He, Exalted is He, said: { Indeed, We sent down the Reminder, and indeed, We will preserve it }, because it pertains to the preservation of the objectives (maqasid) that ensure the well-being of both abodes, which are the necessities, needs, and enhancements, along with what completes and supplements them, and these are the roots of the Sharia. Definitive evidence has been established regarding their consideration, and all branches are based upon them, so there is no doubt that they constitute knowledge, a foundation, firmly established, and stable in its pillars" (4).

(1) Surah Al-Hijr, 9.
(2) Surah Al-Ma'idah, 3.
(3) Al-Shatibi, Al-Muwafaqat, 1/13.
(4) Al-Shatibi, Al-Muwafaqat, 1/43 - 44.
