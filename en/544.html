His third evidence is the induction that the Sharia (Islamic law) was established for the sake of goals or outcomes intended by the Legislator. If we refer back to his concept of benefits (masalih) and harms (mafasid), which is what the Legislator intended through legislation, and to his discussions on causes that were clarified previously, it becomes evident that this evidence here is his proof that the rulings of Allah, Exalted is He, are justified by their objectives. This is what he mentioned at the beginning of the book on the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia), and it is not something close to it or based upon it, but rather it is the same. He said: "The third is the legal evidences and complete induction that the outcomes are considered in the essence of legality, as His saying, { O mankind, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, that you may become righteous } (1). And His saying, { Fasting has been prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, that you may become righteous } (2). And His saying, { And do not consume one another's wealth unjustly or send it [in bribery] to the rulers } (3). And His saying, { And do not insult those whom they invoke besides Allah } (4). And His saying, { Messengers as bringers of good tidings and warners } (5).

__________

(1) Surah Al-Baqarah, 21. The intended meaning is that the command to worship is for the sake of its outcome, which is righteousness.

(2) Surah Al-Baqarah, 183. The intended inference is that Allah commanded fasting for its outcome, which is righteousness.

(3) Surah Al-Baqarah, 188. The complete verse is { so that you may consume a portion of the wealth of the people }. The intended meaning is the prohibition of the act to prevent its outcome, which is the oppression of people and the consumption of their rights.

(4) Surah Al-An'am, 108. The complete verse is { and they insult Allah in enmity without knowledge }. The intended meaning is to prevent the outcome by removing its cause, and the outcome is that they insult Allah, Exalted is He.

(5) Surah An-Nisa, 165. The complete verse is { so that there will be for the people no argument against Allah after the messengers }. The intended meaning is that the sending of messengers has an outcome, which is that there will be no argument for the people against Allah.
