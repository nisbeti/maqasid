And this interpretation of this text is one that observes and considers its methodology and principles that have been established. As for the claim that it is permissible to commit wrong based on the principle of outcomes, this is rejected, and one can refer to al-Shatibi's texts in the discussion of the objectives of the legally responsible (maqasid al-mukallaf), where his views on this matter are clarified in the sixth chapter of this book, where his opinion is that these statements can only arise from ignorance, and if that is not the case, then it is an innovation.

Chapter Nine

On the Application of the Idea:

Three General Principles

The subject of this chapter:

After clarifying al-Shatibi's methodology in understanding the Sharia and his approach in establishing the general principles and rules, which is the beneficial induction leading to certainty, this chapter comes to explain three general principles that are among the most important rules in al-Shatibi's methodology, which are the considered interests, preference, and blocking the means. This is done in three discussions, and the principle of removing harm is examined within the discussion on blocking the means. The aim of discussing these principles is:

1 - To further clarify al-Shatibi's methodology in understanding the Sharia and deriving rulings, as these principles are universals that he established through applying his idea of inductively extracting meanings and capturing universals, as he expressed it. Since these principles are objectives or reasons, and based on them rulings branch out, they are therefore considered principles for the jurist when deriving rulings.
