The idea of the consequences of actions (ma'alat al-af'al) is one of the most important concepts in al-Shatibi's methodology, and many rules are built upon it. In fact, considering the consequences is the primary foundation of his approach. The essence of his idea is that the Sharia (Islamic law) was revealed for the benefit of the servants, thus the Sharia has consequences, and the rulings have consequences. For the sake of these consequences, the Sharia was established, making the Sharia and its rulings causes for certain effects. Furthermore, al-Shatibi's assertion that the actions of Allah (Exalted is He) and His rulings are based on reasoning was derived from a survey of the texts whose explicit meanings indicate the purpose behind the action or ruling. The permissible action is permitted for the sake of a goal intended by the legislator, which is the intended benefit from the ruling. The prohibited action has a consequence or result that the legislator intended to eliminate, which is the harm intended to be removed by the prohibition.
These benefits and harms are the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia), meaning they are the objectives of the Legislator in establishing the Sharia from the outset.
This is a foundation or principle in legislation according to al-Shatibi's methodology. Actions are sought to be performed or abandoned for the sake of their outcomes. Permissible actions may lead to results that are not intended by the Legislator, and likewise, actions that are prohibited may lead to the removal of results that the Legislator did not intend to remove. Therefore, not everything that results from an action is intended by the Legislator. The intended outcome, whether it is to be established or removed, is understood through induction. This has been explained in detail previously. The outcome that is established through beneficial induction to the point of certainty is what the Legislator intends, whether in existence or removal. This is a basis for legislation or a reason for legislation, or a benefit intended by the Legislator through a command, or a harm intended to be removed by prohibition. In summary, these are the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) or the rulings or outcomes of the Sharia or the rulings.
