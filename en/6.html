I intended for this book to be a study of the true concept of the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) according to al-Shatibi. Therefore, its subject is an analytical study of al-Shatibi's ideas, particularly what he has written in the book al-Muwafaqat. This book is an analytical study of the texts of al-Muwafaqat.

In this study, I relied on the edition that was prepared by the two scholars Muhammad al-Khidr Hussein from Tunisia and Muhammad Hasanein Makhlouf. It consists of four volumes in two volumes, where the first scholar prepared the first and second volumes, and the second scholar prepared the third and fourth volumes. Thus, whenever I refer to or cite al-Muwafaqat, I have referred to this edition, and if I refer to another, I will note that.

I have not provided biographical information about the figures in this book except briefly and where I found it necessary, such as the figures who are frequently cited.

I have extracted all the verses that appeared in the book. I have also extracted all the hadiths mentioned in it in a concise manner, relying on the CD of the Noble Hadith Encyclopedia program, produced by Sakhr Company, second edition, which contains the nine books of hadith (mentioned in the list of sources and references), and it relies on global numbering. Whenever I did not find sufficient information in this program, I indicated the book or books from which I derived it, mentioning the name of the book, its edition, the hadith number, the volume, and the page.

I have not included any statement or opinion of al-Shatibi without substantiating it with a text or texts from him, even if this opinion had been clarified in a previous chapter, in order to maintain the completeness of the chapter's unity and its independence as much as possible. Often, I have provided more than one text to indicate al-Shatibi's position or opinion, in order to prevent misinterpretation of his statements.
