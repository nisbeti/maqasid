He said regarding the issue of the prohibition of domesticated donkeys and all predatory animals with fangs: "Indeed, Allah, Exalted is He, has permitted the good things and prohibited the evil things, and between these two principles are matters that can be categorized under one of them. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) clarified this matter. He prohibited the consumption of all predatory animals with fangs and all birds with talons, and he prohibited the consumption of the flesh of domesticated donkeys" (1). He said: "All of this relates to the meaning of being categorized under the principle of evil things, just as the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) categorized the monitor lizard, the bustard, the rabbit, and similar animals under the principle of good things" (2).
