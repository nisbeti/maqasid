However, the interests of the servants may align with the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia), meaning they may serve it, such as the drive to work for earning a livelihood, marriage, fulfilling desires, and responding to human nature. So, are these objectives considered Sharia objectives? That is, is the pursuit of interests among the objectives of the Sharia? He says: "The Sharia objectives are of two types: original objectives and subsidiary objectives. As for the original ones, they are those in which there is an interest for the obligated person, and they are the necessities recognized in every community" (1). Then he said: "As for the subsidiary objectives, they are those in which the interest of the obligated person is taken into account, through which he attains what he is naturally inclined to, such as fulfilling desires, enjoying permissible things, and fulfilling needs" (2).
