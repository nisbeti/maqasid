The meaning of reasoning (istidlal):(1)

As has been clarified, the intended meaning of reasoning here is not its linguistic connotation, but rather it is a term that contrasts with inference (istinbat) and has emerged as a subject and influence in the science of usul al-fiqh (principles of jurisprudence), incorporated into its discussions under the influence of logic and theology. The one who reasons - in this sense - does not derive a ruling based on the indications of texts, but rather imagines certain meanings that he sees as appropriate and proposes them, relying on the possibility of their acceptance by the Sharia in his view and the absence of any evidence that negates them. Therefore, we find al-Juwayni differentiating between the second and third categories mentioned earlier, stating in the first of them: "By referring it to an established origin in which the ruling is in accordance with the view," and in the second: "Without the observer finding an established origin with a consistent ruling that testifies to it, and this is what is called reasoning."

(1) It is mentioned in Lisan al-Arab by Ibn Manzur: "And the proof (dalil): what one uses to reason. The proof is the indicator - and it has guided him to the path, guiding him with indication and evidence, and the opening is the highest." 11/248.
