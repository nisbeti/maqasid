And among them is that the legislator differentiates between two matters in ruling by mentioning a characteristic; this indicates that this characteristic is the reason for the differentiation. If it had not been mentioned, the differentiation would not have existed, and the ruling would have included both matters. This is like His saying, Exalted is He: { And do not approach them until they are pure } (1). The legislator's differentiation between the two matters, purity and menstruation, in the permissibility of approaching them indicates the reason for the prohibition, which is menstruation. This differentiation is based on the term of limitation (until). And like the saying of the Prophet - peace and blessings be upon him -: "Gold for gold, silver for silver, wheat for wheat, barley for barley, dates for dates, and salt for salt, equal for equal, hand to hand; if these types differ, then sell as you wish, provided it is hand to hand" (2). The legislator's differentiation between the two matters, which are the same kind and their difference, in the permissibility or prohibition of selling these types indicates the reason for the prohibition, which is the sameness of the kind.
