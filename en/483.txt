The second: He said: "Among them is the well-known aspect among scholars, as in the following hadiths, regarding what has been briefly mentioned of the rulings, whether according to the ways of action, its causes, its conditions, its impediments, or its attachments, or similar matters, such as the clarification of the prayers with their various timings, bowing, prostration, and all their rulings, and the clarification of zakat in its amounts, timings, the designated zakatable assets, and specifying what is zakatable and what is not" (1) etc. This meaning falls under the meaning intended by al-Shatibi, as it is an explanation and elaboration of the general.
The third: This is the meaning intended by al-Shatibi, which is the return of the Sunnah to comprehensive Qur'anic meanings that all fall under necessities, needs, improvements, or complements of any of them (2).
