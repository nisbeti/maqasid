Al-Shatibi says: "For the legislator did not intend to impose burdens that cause hardship in it" (1) and he presented evidence through useful inductive reasoning, including His saying, Exalted is He: { And He relieves them of their burden and the shackles that were upon them } (2), and His saying: { Our Lord, and lay not upon us a burden like that which You laid upon those before us } (3), and His saying: { No soul is charged except with that within its capacity } (4), and His saying: { Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship } (5), and His saying: { And He has not made upon you in religion any difficulty } (6), and His saying: { Allah intends to lighten for you, and mankind was created weak } (7), { Allah does not intend to make difficulty for you, but He intends to purify you } (8), and in the hadith: "I was sent with the easy, lenient religion" (9), and "Whenever two options are presented to him, he should choose the easier one as long as it is not sinful" (10), and other evidences (11). He said: "If He had intended hardship, He would not have intended ease or relief, but would have intended difficulty and hardship, which is false" (12),
