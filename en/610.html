The First Topic

A Brief Presentation of the Concept of Maqasid According to al-Shatibi

The Meaning of al-Muwafaqat:

The choice of the name al-Muwafaqat (The Concurrences) refers to a meaning intended by al-Shatibi, which is the reconciliation between the schools of thought by demonstrating that although they differ in branches, they all revert to recognized principles and foundations among all. The reason for the disagreement is the differing opinions of the imams in tracing issues back to their origins; one prefers to trace it back to one origin while another prefers a different one. However, all the origins are considered valid and accepted by the imams. Despite the novelty and excitement in his approach, he states that it is recognized by the esteemed imams even if they did not mention it or draw attention to it, and thus, it should not be dismissed by those who have not been exposed to it on the grounds that they have not heard of its like. As for the meaning of reconciling between the schools of thought, he indicated this by mentioning a striking story. He said: "And because of the secrets of obligation related to this noble Sharia that I have embedded in it, I named it 'The Title of Introduction to the Secrets of Obligation.' Then I moved away from this sign to a strange chain that astonishes the perceptive and astute. The essence of it is that one day I met some of the scholars whom I had allowed to take the place of benefiting from me, and I made their scientific gatherings a place for my journey and a venue for my visitation. I had begun to arrange and classify the book, casting aside distractions without refining or composing it. He said to me, 'I saw you last night in a dream with a book you authored. I asked you about it, and you informed me that it is (The Book of al-Muwafaqat).' He said: 'I was going to ask you about the meaning of this delightful title, and you would tell me that you reconciled between the schools of Ibn al-Qasim and Abu Hanifa.' I said to him, 'You have indeed hit the target with a shot from the righteous vision, and you have taken from the prophetic glad tidings a good portion and share, for I have begun to compile these meanings, intending to establish those foundations, for they are the recognized principles among scholars and the foundations upon which the ancients built.' The scholar was astonished by the strangeness of this agreement, just as I was astonished by traversing this wilderness and accompanying these companions."(1)
