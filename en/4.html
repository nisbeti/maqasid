Those whose efforts have gone astray in this worldly life while they think they are doing good – in the best of cases – said: "Leave these people alone, for they are stagnant, they do not evolve, nor do they innovate, and they do not understand reality. Indeed, there are secrets in the Sharia (law) and priorities and balances in the religion. When these texts of the Qur'an and the Sunnah confront them, they become confused and evasive, they search and dig, then they take snippets and say." Among the things they said were: "The interests (masalih) and the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia), al-Shatibi and the Muwafaqat of al-Shatibi, the Muwafaqat: the masterpiece of al-Shatibi, the Sheikh of the objectives." They took snippets and then had the audacity to say: "Those who confront us with the texts are still governed by the complex of the text, and they delude themselves that they are people of jurisprudence (fiqh) while others are merely literalists." What audacity is this! And what kind of jurisprudence is this! And among whom do they rush!

The researcher examined these statements and opinions.

The interests (masalih), and the unregulated interests (masalih mursala)! That's fine, let it be, but what you have brought forth is neither interests nor unregulated interests; it is, in the terminology of usul al-fiqh, nullified interests, contradicted by the texts, so they are, therefore, corruptions (mafasid).

The objectives, the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia)! Yes, let it be, but what you have brought forth is not the objectives of the Sharia; it is your objectives, or the objectives of the sick-hearted. So where in the objectives of the Sharia does the preservation of religion become the freedom of belief and the right to change one's religion? And where in the objectives of the Sharia is the abrogation of the Sharia, so that apostasy becomes a human right, and the killing of an apostate becomes a crime? And is it in the objectives of the Sharia that the text states: { and He has forbidden usury } (1) and the objective is: usury is permissible, with time and place being sufficient for that!

And where in the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) does the preservation of wealth mean the freedom of ownership, and the preservation of the intellect mean the freedom of opinion? Is this in our religion, we Muslims?
