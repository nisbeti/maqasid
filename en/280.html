It indicates the permissibility of waking up in a state of ritual impurity (janabah) and the validity of fasting, because the allowance for intimacy until dawn necessitates this, even if it is not the intended point of clarification, as it is a necessary consequence of the intention to clarify the permissibility of intimacy, eating, and drinking"(1).

Al-Shatibi says in his response to this statement when presenting the evidence of those who prohibit the secondary meaning from the indication of the word: "As for the duration of menstruation, it is not accepted that the hadith indicates it, and there is a dispute regarding it. Therefore, the Hanafis say that its maximum is ten days, and even if it is accepted, it is not from the perspective of the indication of the word by convention, and there is discussion about it. The issue of al-Shafi'i regarding the impurity of water is from the perspective of analogy (qiyas) or otherwise. The minimum duration of pregnancy is derived from the first perspective, not the second. Likewise, the issue of waking up in a state of ritual impurity"(2).
