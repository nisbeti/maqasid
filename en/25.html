Al-Shatibi says: "When I wanted to adhere to the straight path, I found myself a stranger among the majority of the people of my time" (1). He also says: "I hesitated between following the Sunnah on the condition of opposing what people had become accustomed to... I saw that destruction in following the Sunnah is salvation, and that people would not avail me anything from Allah... The Day of Judgment arose upon me, and blame poured upon me, and the arrows of reproach were directed at me, and I was attributed to innovation (bid'ah) and misguidance, and I was placed in the rank of the ignorant and the foolish... At times, I was accused of opposing the allies of Allah... and at times, I was accused of opposing the Sunnah and the community" (3).

(1) Al-Shatibi, al-I'tisam, 1/18.

(2) Fawwaq: to throw. Al-Fayruzabadi said: "We threw upwards: a shot." Al-Qamus al-Muhit, p. 1187.

(3) Al-Shatibi, al-I'tisam, 1/18-21.
