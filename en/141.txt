Al-Amidi said: "The purpose of the legislation of rulings... cannot be anything other than being among the necessary objectives (maqasid al-daruriyyah) or not among the necessary objectives. If it is among the necessary objectives, then it is either a fundamental principle or not a fundamental principle. If it is a fundamental principle, it pertains to the five objectives that no community or law has ever neglected, which are: the preservation of religion, life, intellect, lineage, and wealth. The preservation of these five objectives is among the necessities and represents the highest ranks of appropriateness. The limitation to these five types is based on reality and the knowledge of the absence of any necessary objective outside of them in practice... And if it is not a fundamental principle, it is the supplementary aspect that complements the necessary objective, such as the emphasis on preserving intellect by prohibiting even a small amount of intoxicants that lead to larger quantities... And if the objective is not among the necessary objectives, it is either something that people need or something that people do not need. If it is something that people need, then it is either a fundamental principle or not a fundamental principle. If it is a fundamental principle, it is the second category that pertains to additional needs, such as the authority of a guardian to marry off a minor not due to a necessity that compels it, but out of need... And if it is not a fundamental principle, it is the supplementary aspect that serves as a completion and fulfillment of the second category, such as observing compatibility and the customary dowry in the marriage of a minor, as it leads to the continuity of marriage and the fulfillment of its objectives... And if the objective is not among the additional needs, it is the third category, which pertains to improvement and adornment and the observance of the best practices of customs and transactions, such as depriving slaves of the capacity to bear witness, as a slave is of lower status and rank because he is subjugated to the owner and occupied with serving him, thus it is not befitting for him to hold the position of testimony due to its honor and great significance, following what people are accustomed to and consider among the virtues of customs" (1).
