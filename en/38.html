And I ask for success and assistance ... in this matter from our Lord, Exalted is He.

This composition was created in the year 820 AH (1). The author did not mention his name, and therefore attributing it to Abu Yahya is not certain (2).

What draws attention to the lack of care is that this composition remains in manuscript form and has not been published in full. As for the attention of contemporary scholars to the book (al-Muwafaqat), Abu Ubaida Mashhoor bin Hassan Al Salman mentioned in his introduction to his commentary on (al-Muwafaqat) that he came across three abridgments of the book:

The first is: (al-Maraafiq 'ala al-Muwafaqat) by Mustafa bin Muhammad Fadl bin Mameen, who passed away in 1328 AH - 1910 CE. It is a composition with a very brief explanation, and this composition was printed along with its explanation in the year 1324 AH in Tunisia (3).

The second is: (Ikhtisar al-Muwafaqat) by Ibrahim bin Tahir bin As'ad Al Azhm (who passed away in 1377 AH - 1957 CE). It is a manuscript in two volumes held by the author's family (4).

The third is: "Tawdeeh al-Mushkilat fi Ikhtisar al-Muwafaqat" by Muhammad Yahya bin Omar Al Mukhtar bin Talib Abdullah Al Walati Al Shinqiti, who passed away in 1330 AH - 1912 CE (5).
