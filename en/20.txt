The reports indicating the loss of wealth and savings in Andalusia are numerous, and this situation that the economic condition has reached indicates a decline and deterioration, but does not indicate famine. Therefore, these conditions do not prevent the existence of wealth and luxury in society. Professor Abu al-Ajfan says: "Despite this tumultuous situation, a kind of extravagance spreads, along with a penchant for jewelry among the wealthy elite, and an artistry in adornment and extravagance in the forms of ornaments among women, as well as a passion for music that becomes widespread even in the shops frequented by the youth"(1).
Scientific Condition:
The level of scholarship and the prevailing cultural climate in Granada during the life of al-Shatibi was good and flourishing, and it did not decline significantly and suddenly except with the fall of Granada and its submission to its enemy. However, it is noted that this level gradually declined in accordance with the political situation. Whenever the rulers had a vision of expansion and the spread of Islam, this was followed by the dissemination and expansion of Islamic sciences, along with a cultural movement. Wherever the rulers exerted effort to maintain stability or the existing situation within their borders, and to maintain a steady level of influence and interaction, this condition was followed by stagnation. This means that the movement of scientific production and the cultural movement in society would stabilize. This was the case in Granada, had it not become a destination and gathering place for scholars coming from all parts of Andalusia, which led to an interaction that affected scientific, intellectual, cultural, industrial, and artistic activity.
