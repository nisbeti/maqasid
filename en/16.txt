Lisan al-Din ibn al-Khatib (1) said: "After al-Ghalib, by the command of Allah, the founder of the state, his son and namesake Sultan Abu Abdullah took over, and his reign lasted until he died in 701 AH. After him, his son and namesake Abu Abdullah Muhammad took over, but he was deposed on the day of Eid al-Fitr in 708 AH, and he died in Shawwal of 711. After him, his brother Nasr Abu al-Juyush took over, but his situation became chaotic, and he sought power from his uncle's son Sultan Abu al-Walid Ismail, who seized the governor's house on the second of Dhul-Qi'dah in 713 AH. Nasr was deposed and moved to the city of Wadi Ash, where he died in 722 AH. The reign of Sultan Abu al-Walid continued until the 23rd of Rajab in 725, when he was attacked by his cousin along with a group of his relatives, and they killed him at his door. Their efforts were in vain, and they were all killed that day. His son Muhammad took over and continued until Dhul-Hijjah of 734, when he was killed at the foot of Jabal al-Fath by his troops from the Maghreb. After him, his brother Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf took over, and his reign lasted until the day of Eid al-Fitr in 755" (2), and he was killed during his prayer (3).

(3) The same source.
