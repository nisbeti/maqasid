As for istihsan (juridical preference) by consensus, it is exemplified by istisna (manufacturing contract). The point intended here is that the analogy (qiyas) in question implies the prohibition of istisna, but consensus (ijma) has indicated its permissibility, thus this is a specification by consensus. It is also said here that there is no analogy stronger than analogy itself; rather, there is a consensus that specifies the ruling. It is stated in Kashf al-Asrar: "Among the istihsan is what has been established by consensus, such as istisna, meaning in matters where people engage in transactions, like when a person orders someone to make him a shoe, for example, for a certain price, specifying its characteristics and size, without mentioning a deadline, and whether he hands over the money or not, it is permissible. The analogy implies its prohibition because it is a sale of something non-existent in the present, and it is non-existent in description in the liability. It is not permissible to sell something except after it has been specified in reality, that is, its establishment in liability like salam (forward sale). As for the absence from every aspect, a contract cannot be conceived, but they preferred to leave it by consensus established through the transactions of the community without objection."(1) This is also a specification, and it is not a specification of an analogy but rather of a generality. What is confirmed from this is that istihsan is a specification, and since specification is nothing but a conflict between generality and specificity, the action is taken according to the stronger of the conflicting parties. Therefore, istihsan is acting according to the stronger evidence, which is the specific one. Since it is an action based on the specific and a disregard for the general, it separates the issue from its counterparts and judges it with the specific ruling. The precedence of the specific effect over the general is istihsan. From this, it may be said: the abandonment of the evidence, meaning the general evidence, in favor of istihsan, which may lead one who does not scrutinize to think that it is an abandonment of the legal evidence in favor of personal opinion, but this is not the case at all. It was said: "If it is said: the consensus has opposed the text, which is his saying (peace and blessings be upon him): 'Do not sell what you do not have.'"(2) We say: the text has become specific in regard to this ruling by consensus, so the analogy negating permissibility remains in opposition to the consensus, thus its consideration is nullified by the opposition of the consensus."(3)
