Therefore, al-Shatibi refutes all the statements of his predecessors on this matter, saying that if one observes the consideration of the Sharia for the purposes of the servants and their inclinations, and what aligns with what they strive for, this is incidental and not the essence. The essence is that Allah - Exalted is He - created man for His worship, but He favored them by assisting them in their worship by creating in them desires and inclinations that drive them towards obedience. He said: "If it is said: The establishment of laws is either in vain or for wisdom, the first is invalid by consensus" (1), and he continued in his statement: "Because every rational person seeks the benefit of himself and what aligns with his desires in this world and the Hereafter, and the Sharia has guaranteed them this demand within the framework of obligation. So how can it be denied that the Sharia is established in accordance with the purposes of the servants and the motivations of their desires? Moreover, it has already been clarified that the Sharia came in accordance with the purposes of the servants and confirmed their shares" (2).
