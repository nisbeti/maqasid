He said: "As for the interests of the two abodes (darain) and their causes and harms, they can only be known through the Sharia, and if something is hidden from that, it should be sought from the evidences of the Sharia, which are the Book, the Sunnah, consensus (ijma), sound analogy (qiyas), and correct reasoning. As for the interests of this world and their causes and harms, they are known through necessities, experiences, customs, and credible conjectures. If something is hidden from that, it should be sought from its evidences. Whoever wishes to know the correspondences, interests, and harms, and which are preferred and which are not, should present that to his intellect, assuming that the Sharia did not address it, and then build upon that the rulings, for hardly any ruling will fall outside of that except what Allah has made obligatory upon His servants without informing them of its benefit or harm."(1)
