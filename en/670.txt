One of them is like His saying, Exalted is He: {Fasting has been prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, that you may attain piety} (1). This text indicates the intention of achieving piety, and piety itself is the fear of Allah, obedience to Him, and not opposing His command while seeking His pleasure. This intention does not return except to control, and there is nothing in it that indicates that the rulings are justified; it is not something upon which that can be built. Among this is also His saying, Exalted is He: {And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me} (2). Such a statement also does not return to establishing an intention or purpose upon which the justification of rulings can be built, neither in a way that, if presented to minds, would be accepted, nor through causes or outcomes.
The second is like His saying, Exalted is He: {To test you}, {So that there will not be}, {So that it will not be for the people}. These indications do not contain anything suitable to be built upon as a fundamental justification for rulings. They are news from the Legislator that conveys the obligation of worship and piety and that they should strive in that, warning them that they will have no excuse on the Day of Judgment regarding what has reached them from the message. So how can one connect and derive the justification of rulings from such texts?!
Sheikh Muhammad al-Tahir ibn Ashour has deemed all of al-Shatibi's arguments on this matter as invalid except for two. So what kind of induction remains after that?
