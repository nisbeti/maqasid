Issues or Branches of the Principle of Removing Harm

When the principle of blocking the means (sadd al-dhara'i) and its regulations are clarified, as well as its area of application, which is the permissible (mubah), it follows that what is in the category of permissibles includes that which is at the level of concession (afw). It does not apply to the recommended (mandubat), and not every change that affects rulings falls under the category of blocking the means. However, it is worth mentioning the actions that al-Shatibi has associated with blocking the means, which include the necessity of abandoning the recommended or supererogatory acts and the necessity of performing the disliked (makruh) in certain cases, and this is in the case of the jurist (faqih) who is to be followed, so that the follower does not mistakenly understand that the continuous practice of the recommended or supererogatory acts without interruption is an obligation, nor that the continuous abandonment of the disliked is forbidden. This is to block the means, and he has established principles regarding this. He said: "The essence of the recommended is that it should not be equated with the obligatory" (1). He said: "The permissibles, in terms of their essence, should not be equated with the recommended or the disliked" (2). He said: "The disliked, in terms of their essence, should not be equated with the forbidden or with the permissibles" (3). It should not be said here that the principle has been applied to the recommended or the disliked; rather, the application of the principle here is focused on the continuity that may unintentionally suggest otherwise to the legislator.
