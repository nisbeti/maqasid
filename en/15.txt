The Spaniards began to wreak havoc and destruction in the lands of Andalusia. Despite all this, Granada remained strong, and peace treaties were concluded between the Muslims and the Spaniards, including a peace treaty with Pedro IV in the year 736 - 789 AH, 1336 - 1387 CE. Security and peace prevailed in the kingdom of Granada, and it flourished. Despite the Spaniards' attempts to divide the properties of Granada, they failed(1).
In the year 867 AH, Gibraltar fell into the hands of the Castilians, which was a severe blow to the Muslims, and the flow of supplies from Morocco to Andalusia ceased(2).
In the year 887 AH - 1482 CE, Muhammad ibn Ali, nicknamed Abu Abdullah al-Saghir, took over the rule of Granada, who agreed to surrender Granada in the year 897 AH - 1492 CE to the monarchs of Castile and Aragon, and he departed to Morocco. Thus, the Islamic state in Andalusia was brought to an end, and it was said about Abu Abdullah al-Saghir (the light-hearted):

Weep like the women for a kingdom that was multiplied...
You did not preserve it like the men.

Political Situation:
This was a reference to historical milestones and events that the Muslims of Andalusia went through. These events explain the migration of the al-Shatibi family to Granada. They also clarify the political situation of the era of al-Shatibi, but there are events that shed greater light on the political situation of the era of al-Shatibi, that is, the eighth century AH.
