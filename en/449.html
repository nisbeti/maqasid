But the interpretation of the Qur'an is a noble and serious task, and it should only be undertaken by those who are qualified for it. Those who are qualified must only speak based on knowledge and clarity, while being cautious of error, for speaking is in the clarification of the intent of Allah, Exalted is He. He said: "Be cautious in speaking about the Book of Allah, Exalted is He, except with clear evidence, for people are in knowledge of the tools needed for interpretation in three categories: the first is those who have reached the level of the firmly rooted in knowledge, such as the Companions (Sahaba) and the Followers (Tabi'in) and those who come after them. These individuals spoke with caution, restraint, reverence, and fear of making mistakes; thus, we are more deserving of this than they are if we think that we possess knowledge like theirs, and how far from that we are!"(1)
