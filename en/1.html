Introduction

All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds, and peace and blessings be upon our master Muhammad, and upon his family and companions altogether, and upon those who follow them in goodness until the Day of Judgment.

I bear witness that there is no deity but Allah alone, without partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, who conveyed the message and fulfilled the trust, and bore witness to the nation and made Allah witness to that. He said: "O Allah, have I conveyed (the message)?" They said: Yes. He said: "O Allah, bear witness," three times they said: Yes, they bear witness for themselves, and he (peace and blessings be upon him) bears witness against them, then Allah, Exalted is He, bears witness to that. Then he (peace and blessings be upon him) joined the highest companion, and the companions after him carried the trust and fulfilled it in the best manner, and they conveyed it to those after them, and the truth appeared and falsehood vanished, Islam prevailed and darkness was dispelled.

Then there came after them a generation that neglected the trust and thus lost themselves; they exchanged fire for light, so they burned and did not find illumination. They forgot Allah, so He forgot them, and caused them to forget themselves; they became deaf while they could hear, their eyes see but they are blind and do not perceive, they have hearts with which they desire but they do not understand, and their chests are constricted.
