Based on al-Shatibi's clarifications, the derived meaning (ma'na tab'i) can be defined as a meaning that serves the original meaning but is not one of the indications of speech. The meaning becomes clearer when al-Shatibi responds to those who assert that the derived meaning is one of the indications of speech. He cites their statement, saying: "They argued that the maximum duration of menstruation is fifteen days based on his saying (peace and blessings be upon him), 'One of you remains in her menstruation for half of her lifetime' (1), and the intended meaning is to inform about the deficiency in religion, not to inform about the maximum duration, but exaggeration necessitated mentioning that, and if the increase had been mentioned, it would have been addressed. Al-Shafi'i argued for the impurity of a small amount of water that is not altered based on his saying (peace and blessings be upon him), 'If one of you awakens from his sleep, he should not dip his hand into the vessel until he washes it' (2)"(3). He then said: "As for their argument regarding the minimum duration of pregnancy being six months, taken from His saying, Exalted is He, { And his gestation and weaning is thirty months } (4), along with His saying, { And weaning is in two years } (5)"(6). He then said: "And they said regarding His saying, Exalted is He, { So now go in unto them } (7), up to His saying, { until the white thread of dawn becomes distinct to you from the black thread } (8), the verse.
