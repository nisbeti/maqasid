He said: "This is a very apparent problem since the context of the verse and the gathered evidence within it indicate that the partners (andad) are the idols or others that they used to worship, and they did not worship themselves nor take them as lords" (1). He then commented: "However, there is a valid interpretation, as he did not say that this is the interpretation of the verse, but rather brought forth what is a partner in legal consideration that the Qur'an has testified to from two aspects. One of them is that the observer may derive from the meaning of the verse a meaning from the perspective of legal consideration and apply it to something that it was not revealed about because it shares the same intent or is similar to it, as the essence of a partner is that which opposes its counterpart, and the commanding self (nafs al-ammara) is of this nature" (2).
Thus, the meaning of consideration in his statement: "Its source is general by consideration" is to apply the meaning of the verse to something that shares the intent with the thing it was revealed about or is similar to it; this is from the perspective of the apparent meaning, while the problem from the perspective of the hidden meaning does not exist in this example. This is that the meaning which fulfills the conditions of the apparent does not revert to a general principle or does not have a witness by consideration, and it cannot be said to pause and not apply the text, because it is a Qur'anic text and upon it is built a legal obligation, thus applying it is obligatory and neglecting it is not valid.
