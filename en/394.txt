The rulings that can be reversed are those that exist or are lifted based on causes, conditions, or impediments, such that the accountable person (mukallaf) has a hand in creating or removing these causes, conditions, or impediments. If he does not have a hand in them because they are beyond his capacity, then legal stratagems (hiyal) do not apply, because a stratagem is an action of the accountable person. Al-Shatibi said: "The actions that occur in existence which necessitate matters for which they are legislated or established fall into two categories. One is beyond the capacity of the accountable person, and the other can be included within his capacity. The former can be a cause, a condition, or an impediment" (1). A cause is like the necessity that permits the consumption of carrion, the fear of hardship that permits the marriage of female slaves, the incontinence that exempts one from the obligation of ablution for each prayer in the presence of an external factor, and the setting or rising of the sun or the dawn that necessitates those prayers and similar matters. Conditions are like the passing of a year being a condition for the obligation of zakat, and reaching puberty being a condition for accountability in general, and the ability to deliver being a condition for the validity of a sale, and maturity being a condition for managing an orphan's wealth, and sending messengers being a condition for reward and punishment, and matters of that nature. An impediment is like menstruation being an impediment to sexual intercourse, divorce, circumambulation of the House, the obligation of prayers, and fasting, and insanity being an impediment to performing acts of worship and exercising control over affairs, and similar matters.
