The response of al-Amidi to these two objections is: "From three aspects: First: The ability only pertains to creation and existence, and nothing else; disbelief and types of sins and evils are related to opposing the prohibition of the Lawgiver, and that is not related to ability in any way. Second: Even if we concede that all of that is created by Allah, we do not claim that wisdom necessarily accompanies His actions in all cases, so that it applies to every created thing; rather, we only claim that in matters where wisdom can be considered, which is possible in all but types of evils and sins, and we do not claim that definitively but rather apparently. Third: Even if we concede that wisdom necessarily accompanies His actions in all cases, we do not concede the impossibility of that in the scenarios they mentioned, as it is possible that the necessary consequence has a ruling known only to the Lord, Exalted is He." (1)
Among the examples of these objections: "The sixteenth: Wisdom is only sought in the case of one whose soul inclines towards bringing benefit or repelling harm, and the Lord, Exalted is He, is free from that. The seventeenth: Wisdom is only sought in the action of one whose action, if devoid of wisdom, would incur blame and be considered frivolous. The Lord is above that, as He manages His kingdom as He wills and chooses without being questioned about what He does. As He said, { He will not be questioned about what He does, but they will be questioned } (2), and even if His action does not necessarily entail wisdom, it is still what is sought." (3)
And al-Amidi responded to these last two objections: "To the sixteenth by denying what they mentioned regarding the consideration of wisdom; rather, wisdom is only sought in the action of one for whom, if wisdom were found in his action, it would not be impossible but rather likely in most cases. And to the seventeenth, what they mentioned only applies to one for whom it is necessary to consider wisdom in his action, and the Creator, Exalted is He, is not like that according to what is indicated in our theological texts." (4)
