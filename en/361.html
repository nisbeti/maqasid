Upon closer examination, we notice a distinction in the linguistic usage in that the term "intention" (niyyah) is more specific than the term "purpose" (qasd). For example, we have observed the scholars saying: "the purpose of the legislator" (maqsad al-shari), "the purposes of the legislator" (maqasid al-shari), and "the purposes of the Sharia" (maqasid al-sharia), but they did not say "the intention of the legislator" or "the legislator's intentions" or "the intentions of the Sharia." Likewise, they did not say "the will of the Sharia" (1).

(1) The term "will" (irada) as it appears in the texts of the scholars means purpose and intention, and it has come in many legal texts. Allah, Exalted is He, said: { Among you are some who want this world, and among you are some who want the Hereafter. } { Al-Imran: 152 }, and He said: { You desire the pleasures of this world, but Allah desires the Hereafter. } { Al-Anfal: 67 }, and He said: { Whoever desires the life of this world and its adornment. } { Hud: 15 }, and He said: { Whoever desires to harvest the Hereafter. } { Ash-Shura: 20 }, and He said: { And do not drive away those who call upon their Lord morning and evening, desiring His face. } { Al-An'am: 52 }, and He said: { And keep yourself patient with those who call upon their Lord morning and evening, desiring His face, and let not your eyes pass beyond them, desiring the adornment of the life of this world. } { Al-Kahf: 28 }, and He said: { And whatever you give in usury to increase within the wealth of the people will not increase with Allah, but whatever you give in zakat, desiring the face of Allah, those are the multipliers. } { Ar-Rum: 39 }. It may also be expressed in the Qur'an with the term "seeking" (ibtigha) as in His saying: { Except seeking the face of his Lord, the Most High. } { Al-Lail: 20 }, and His saying: { And the example of those who spend their wealth seeking the pleasure of Allah and assuring themselves. } { Al-Baqarah: 265 }, and His saying: { And you spend nothing except seeking the face of Allah. } { Al-Baqarah: 272 }. See Jam' al-Ulum wal-Hikam by Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali, p. 17.
