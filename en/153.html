And he concluded with the statement: "It is established that the reasoning behind God's rulings - Exalted is He - based on public interests (masalih) is invalid. This statement, while it is an objection to what they have said, is also a decisive indication starting from the issue. Through this, the invalidity of all the arguments they relied upon becomes apparent, as they are speculative evidence, while what we have mentioned is a conclusive proof. Then we say: If what you have mentioned indicates that the reasoning behind God's actions - Exalted is He - based on public interests is valid, then we have conclusive evidence that prevents this, and it is from the following aspects:" (1) He then mentioned these aspects, all of which pertain to the essence of God, and have no relation to the science of usul al-fiqh (principles of jurisprudence). Among these aspects he mentioned: "First: He is the Creator of the actions of the servants, and this prevents the claim that He - Exalted is He - considers public interests." (2) "The second proof: that it is not permissible to reason God's actions - Exalted is He - and His rulings based on public interests, is that if one is capable of disbelief but not capable of faith, it necessitates compulsion, and this undermines the consideration of public interests." (3) "The third proof: that there has been a command to do what is beyond one's capacity, and this prevents the claim of considering public interests." (4) Thus, all the aspects or proofs he presented up to the eighth one, where he said: "As for God - Exalted and Glorified is He - since He is entirely free from public interests and harms, and we see that the majority of His actions do not serve the interests of creation, how can it be presumed that His actions and rulings are reasoned based on public interests?!" (5).
