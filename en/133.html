This debate between the two parties, one presenting evidence and the other refuting it, weakens the position of those who adopt the approach of analogy (qiyas) and reasoning. Their weak position can be summarized in one point, which is that considering a specific description in a particular ruling and claiming it as a cause is an arbitrary judgment without evidence. Consequently, it relies on mere rational conjecture, which is not a legal conjecture. This leads to the weakness of their position in reasoning, as it is based on considering appropriate descriptions in numerous rulings. The refuters said: "The utmost that can be said in this regard is that the texts may align with the description, resulting in numerous parallels, and with numerous parallels, strength in the description is not achieved." (1)
