If the consideration is for a description that is broader than the description to which the ruling has testified, and for a ruling that is broader than the ruling that has testified to the description, then it is the consideration of the genus of the description in the genus of the ruling. An example of this type is what al-Amidi mentioned: "The consideration of the genus of the common hardship between the menstruating woman and the traveler in the genus of alleviation, for the specific hardship of the menstruating woman is not the same as the specific hardship of the traveler, but rather of its genus, and the specific alleviation for the traveler by the omission of two units of prayer is not the same as the alleviation for the menstruating woman by the omission of prayer, but rather of its genus" (1).
Accordingly, the consideration in the testimony of the ruling to the description has four types (2):
1- Consideration of the specific description in the specific ruling.
2- Consideration of the genus of the description in the specific ruling.
3- Consideration of the specific description in the genus of the ruling.
4- Consideration of the genus of the description in the genus of the ruling.
