The lack of political thought is a harbinger of the end of any state and the destruction of any nation, no matter how powerful it may be (1). It goes without saying that political thought cannot exist without the existence of political theory, system, and legislation upon which this thought and thinking are based. However, even if laws, systems, and legislations exist—as is the case—if there is no thought regarding responsibility for the nation and its issues and the management of its affairs based on these laws and legislations—this is political thought—then their existence is equivalent to their non-existence.
