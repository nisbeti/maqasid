He says based on the principle of the prohibition of preference: "The neglect of this principle has led many followers of the jurists to give fatwas to their relatives or friends that they do not give to others, following their own desires and whims or the desires of that relative or friend. This has been observed in past times, let alone in our own time, as has the pursuit of the concessions of the schools of thought in accordance with desire and whim" (1). After mentioning examples of these fatwas and condemning them strongly, he said: "This is something upon which there is no disagreement among Muslims who are considered credible in consensus, that it is not permissible, nor is it lawful for anyone to give a fatwa in the religion of Allah except with the truth that he believes to be true, which is accepted by those who accept it and rejected by those who reject it. The mufti is merely a reporter about Allah, Exalted is He, in His rulings, so how can he report except what he believes to be the ruling He has decreed and made obligatory" (2).
