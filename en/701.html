(1) al-Nasr: The chapter of the Qur'an that discusses the victory of Allah and the conquest of Mecca.

(2) al-Nur: The chapter that addresses issues of morality and social conduct.

(3) al-Ma'arij: The chapter that discusses the trials and tribulations faced by believers.

(4) al-Hajj: The chapter that discusses the pilgrimage and the rights of the oppressed.

(5) al-Mu'minun: The chapter that outlines the characteristics of true believers.

(6) al-Jathiya: The chapter that discusses the consequences of following one's desires.

(7) Muhammad: The chapter named after the Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him).

(8) al-Isra: The chapter that discusses the night journey and the importance of prayer.

(9) al-Baqarah: The longest chapter of the Qur'an, covering various aspects of Islamic law and guidance.

(10) al-Lail: The chapter that discusses the contrast between the righteous and the wicked.

(11) Ta-Ha: The chapter that recounts the story of Moses and emphasizes the importance of divine guidance.

(12) al-Tawbah: The chapter that addresses issues of repentance and the consequences of disobedience.

(13) al-Mulk: The chapter that reflects on the creation of the universe and the purpose of life.

(14) al-Baqarah: The chapter that addresses economic ethics, particularly regarding usury.
