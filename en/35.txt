His student al-Majari said about him: "The Sheikh, the Imam, the renowned scholar, a unique fabric and the only one of his era" (1). Imam Ibn Marzouk al-Hafid said about him: "The Sheikh, the professor, the jurist, the accomplished Imam, the righteous scholar" (2). Ahmad Baba al-Tanbukti translated him in Nيل الابتهاج, describing him as: "The Imam, the accomplished scholar, the exemplary, the great memorizer, the diligent mujtahid" (3). He said: "He was a foundational scholar (usuli), a commentator (mufassir), a jurist (faqih), a hadith scholar (muhaddith), a linguist (lugawi), an eloquent speaker (bayani), a steadfast debater (nazar), pious, righteous, ascetic, and a Sunni. He was an absolute Imam, a meticulous researcher, an adept dialectician in the sciences, one of the distinguished and verified scholars, and one of the great Imams who were proficient and trustworthy. He had a firm foundation and supreme leadership in the sciences of jurisprudence, principles, exegesis, hadith, Arabic, and others, with thoroughness and verification... on a solid foundation of righteousness, jurisprudence, thoroughness, and piety" (4).
His Works:
Al-Shatibi has significant works, the most important of which are two: al-Muwafaqat and al-I'tisam, both of which are published. Some of his works have not reached us, and his writings indicate a vast knowledge and comprehensive understanding, as well as precision and insight, self-confidence and independence, the ability to investigate and verify, the pursuit of rare and overlooked matters, and integrity in seeking truth and correctness, while avoiding fanaticism and rigidity.
