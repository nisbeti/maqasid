It has become clear that the objectives (maqasid) are results or causes. The causes are the actions performed by the accountable person (mukallaf) that lead to those results; they serve as means for the objectives. The actions performed by the mukallaf may be legislatively commanded, prohibited, or permitted.
The objectives of the mukallaf from his actions may be compliance with the command of the legislator, meaning they may be otherworldly objectives without regard to a worldly objective. They may also be worldly, that is, the attainment of desires and interests, according to what Allah has instilled in humans of characteristics and passions(1). They may also combine both intentions: the intention of compliance and the intention of seeking benefit.
What is the effect of the mukallaf's intention on the validity of the action, whether in terms of reward or punishment in the Hereafter, or in terms of its occurrence and the resulting effects in this world? Is it permissible for the mukallaf to intend his interests and benefits while also intending compliance? Is it permissible for him to aim for results that Allah did not legislate those actions for? Is it permissible for him to intend legitimate objectives through illegitimate actions? And what is the ruling on actions performed by the mukallaf devoid of any intention?
