His fourth proof: { And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me } (1). This also applies to what was previously mentioned, as it has been presented to prove the purpose or intended result of the legislator from creation { to worship Me }, and it also confirms the aforementioned concept of al-Shatibi regarding the term public interest (maslaha). If what he meant by this term was the description or appropriate meaning that is understood by rational individuals and wise people, then this text would contradict his aim. This text is cited by those who oppose reasoning based on public interests. And where is the interest of the nonexistent in creating him for obligation? There is no reasonable interest, and this affirms the earlier mention of al-Shatibi's intention with the term public interest.

His fifth proof: { He who created death and life to test you which of you is best in deed } (2), and this is essentially the same as his third proof. It has been presented to increase the evidence. What has been said about what preceded it can be said here.

His sixth proof: { Allah does not intend to make difficulty for you, but He intends to purify you and complete His favor upon you } (3). It is also noted that he cited this because it includes mentioning the result that can arise from this obligation { to make }, { to purify you }, { and to complete His favor }. Even if a person recognizes the benefit in cleanliness or purification, he cannot adopt a form of cleanliness in place of the prescribed form. Considering these objectives as public interests indicates that they are regarded as such because they are intended by the legislator, not that Allah, Exalted is He, legislated what the servants perceive as beneficial.
