And it was brought to Professor Ahmad Muhammad Shakir, the verifier of the book Al-Risala, a commentary on the hadith "What comes to you from me, compare it with the Book of Allah; whatever agrees with it, I have said it, and whatever contradicts it, I have not said it." He stated: "This meaning has not been reported in any authentic or good hadith; rather, many phrases have been reported, all of which are fabricated or extremely weak to the extent that none of them is suitable for proof or citation" (1). He then mentioned that among those who reported it are those who are known for fabricating hadith, and he cited the statements of the hadith scholars that it is a false hadith with no basis and that it was fabricated by the heretics and is indeed fabricated (2).
Al-Zarkashi said: "Ibn Abd al-Barr stated in his book Al-Jami' Bayaan al-'Ilm: Abdul Rahman ibn Mahdi said: The heretics and the Khawarij fabricated the hadith 'What comes to you from me, compare it...' " (3). He also said: "Some opposed it and said: We compare it with the Book of Allah and found it contradicting the Book, for we did not find in it that only what agrees with the Book is accepted, but rather we found the command to obey him and the prohibition of opposing his command is a ruling in all cases" (4).
