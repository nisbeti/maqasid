... However, al-Shatibi makes exceptions here for some rare cases, as he did regarding acts of worship. This is because acts of worship are not governed by meanings unless there is a text or consensus indicating a specific meaning. Likewise, in customary practices, meanings are followed unless there is an element of worship; in that case, one must adhere to the texts. He says: "If this is established and the predominant aspect of customary practices is to pay attention to meanings, then if there is an element of worship, one must submit and adhere to the texts, such as the request for the dowry in marriage, the slaughtering in the specified manner for permissible animals, the fixed shares in inheritance, the number of months in the waiting periods for divorce and completion, and similar matters where there is no room for intellects to understand their specific benefits, so that others can be analogized to them"(1).

(1) Al-Shatibi, al-Muwafaqat, 2/214.
