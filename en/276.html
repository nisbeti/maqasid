The traveler feared it, like a monkey... as the ship feared the return of the spring (1).

Omar said: "O people, hold on to the record of your poetry from the time of ignorance, for in it is the interpretation of your Book... If this is the case, then it is necessary to pay attention to understanding the meaning of the address" (2).

This is if the meanings are the meanings of the words and texts; however, if what is meant by meanings are the meanings of rulings, that is, the rulings or presumed interests for them, which, if established through induction, become certain legal interests and objectives (maqasid); and if they are not established as such, they are treated according to their service or destruction of established objectives, and are considered or rejected based on that. In this sense of the term meanings, al-Shatibi discusses whether this is from the indications of language or something for which speech was intended or not, and he concludes that it is not from the indications of language, and this will become clear in what follows.
