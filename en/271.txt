Accordingly, it is impossible to translate the Qur'an and convey it into a non-Arabic tongue, because while the words and meanings of phrases can be transferred to another language, the style has an impact on the meanings, which cannot be translated. However, it is permissible to interpret the Qur'an for the general public and for those who are unable to understand its meanings, and it is permissible to translate the words and phrases, but this is merely a transfer of their meanings without preserving the effect of the style, which provides additional meanings that serve the meanings of the words and phrases (2).
The illiteracy of the Sharia:
Al-Shatibi says: "The illiterate (ummi) is one who is attributed to the mother, remaining on the original state of birth from the mother, having not learned a book or anything else, thus he is in the original state in which he was born" (3). He says: "This Sharia is illiterate because its people are likewise; as for the fact that its people are so, it is decisive due to His saying, Exalted is He: { He it is Who has sent among the unlettered a Messenger from among themselves } (4) and His saying: { So believe in Allah and His Messenger, the unlettered Prophet who believes in Allah and His words } (5), and in the hadith: 'I was sent to an illiterate nation' (6), and also 'We are an illiterate nation; we do not calculate nor write' (7)" (8) and other such evidence.
