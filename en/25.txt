Al-Shatibi says: "When I wanted to adhere to the straight path, I found myself a stranger among the majority of the people of my time"(1). He also says: "I hesitated between following the Sunnah on the condition of opposing what people were accustomed to... I saw that destruction in following the Sunnah is salvation, and that people will not avail me anything from Allah... The Day of Judgment arose upon me, and blame poured upon me, and the arrows(2) of reproach were directed at me, and I was attributed to innovation and misguidance, and placed in the ranks of the ignorant and the foolish... At times, I was accused of opposing the allies of Allah... and at times, I was accused of opposing the Sunnah and the community"(3).
__________
(1) Al-Shatibi, al-I'tisam, 1/18.
(2) Fawwaq: to shoot. Al-Fayruzabadi said: "We shot upwards: to cast." Al-Qamus al-Muhit, p. 1187.
(3) Al-Shatibi, al-I'tisam, 1/18-21.
