The ten possibilities that have been mentioned are not present in every example, and the absence of those that do exist is not impossible. For instance, His Exalted saying: { Establish the prayer } (1) does not necessitate induction to ascertain the certainty of the obligation of prayer. Understanding the transition of the meaning of prayer from its linguistic reality to its legal reality, and other objections that may arise, is not impossible. Likewise, the definitive rulings such as the obligation of faith, fasting, almsgiving, pilgrimage, truthfulness, justice, benevolence, and fulfilling trusts, as well as the prohibition of theft, adultery, usury, the killing of the soul which Allah has forbidden, and the prohibition of carrion, blood, and pork, are definitive and were not established through induction. Rather, they were derived from the texts, supported by the clarification of the Sunnah, the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), consensus, and the transmission of the texts and actions in a mutawatir manner. A single text can be definitively indicative, such as His saying: { Prohibited to you is carrion, blood, and the flesh of swine } (2), and His saying: { And Allah has permitted trade and prohibited usury } (3). If certainty necessitates certainty in the meanings of words or the benefit from certain indications, then this is possible and actual, and not impossible.
It is not correct that texts cannot indicate meanings with certainty, nor is it correct that certainty can only be achieved through induction.
As for the induction of the meanings of rulings, its discussion will come separately under the title: the meaningful transmission (al-tawatur al-ma'nawi).
The application of induction (istiqra) with the aim of reaching legal truths requires defining its meaning and how to apply it through agreed-upon examples or known rulings so that it can be utilized in the search for understanding what is unknown in terms of rulings. This is something that al-Shatibi did not do; rather, he imposed the method of induction without proving its validity and without providing a definition of it.
