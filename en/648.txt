The ten probabilities that have been mentioned are not present in every example, and the absence of those that exist is not impossible. For instance, His saying, Exalted is He: { Establish the prayer } (1) does not necessitate induction to ascertain the certainty of the obligation of prayer. Understanding the transition of the meaning of prayer from its linguistic reality to its legal reality, and other such objections, is not impossible. Likewise, the definitive rulings such as the obligation of faith, fasting, almsgiving, pilgrimage, truthfulness, justice, benevolence, and fulfilling trusts, as well as the prohibition of theft, adultery, usury, the killing of the soul which Allah has forbidden, and the prohibition of carrion, blood, and pork, are all definitive and were not established through induction. Rather, they were derived from the texts, supported by the clarification of the Sunnah, the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), consensus, and the widely transmitted texts and actions. A single text can be definitively indicative, such as His saying, Exalted is He: { Prohibited to you is carrion, blood, and the flesh of swine } (2), and His saying, Exalted is He: { And Allah has permitted trade and prohibited usury } (3). If certainty is required for the sake of certainty, certainty regarding the meanings of words or benefiting from certain contextual clues, then this is possible and actual, and not impossible.
It is not correct to say that texts cannot indicate definitively the meanings, nor is it correct to say that certainty can only be achieved through induction. As for the induction of the meanings of rulings, it will be discussed separately under the title: the meaningful transmission (tawatur al-ma'nawi). The application of induction with the aim of reaching legal truths requires defining its meaning and how to apply it through agreed-upon or well-known rulings so that it can be applied in the search for knowledge of what is unknown in ruling. This is something that al-Shatibi did not do; rather, he imposed the method of induction without proving its validity and without defining it.
