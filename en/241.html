Similarly, the matter regarding the ranks of the objectives (maqasid) is as follows: the necessities are what the legislator has requested and repeated his request or emphasized it to a certain degree, while the needs are what the legislator has requested and confirmed to a certain degree without reaching the first level. Likewise, what the legislator has requested in a manner that places it at a lower rank is called enhancements.

From this perspective, the second aspect, al-Shatibi divided the objectives into three categories: necessary, needful, and enhancing. Here are a few clear and decisive texts from him that illustrate his methodology in this matter to clarify the rank of the objective within these categories or its rank within a single category.

He says: "Indeed, before the rise of this light (1), we were groping about like the blind, and our minds were running in pursuit of our interests in a disordered manner, due to their weakness in bearing these burdens, and the participation of hasty desires... So we place poisons in the place of remedies (2)... and we derive barren analogies, and we seek the signs of health from a sick body, and we walk on our faces while we think we are walking on the straight path, until the pure compulsion appeared in the face of destiny and the reality of the hands of necessity was raised to the One, the All-Compelling" (3).

__________

(1) This light refers to Islam.

(2) That is, we consider an interest to be a harm and a harm to be an interest.

(3) Al-Shatibi, al-Muwafaqat, 1/2. That is, Islam came and clarified the interests and harms, and the reality of human incapacity and their need for someone to clarify that for them, and that is only Allah.
