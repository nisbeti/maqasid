The limitations and shortcomings of human intellect, along with its inability to comprehend the essence of Allah and to investigate the unseen, prevent it from grasping Allah's wisdom in His creation and rulings without revelation from Him, Exalted is He. A person may perceive what is beneficial as harmful and what is harmful as beneficial, and may see what contradicts wisdom. If one were to compare the unseen to the tangible, they might arrive at conclusions that contradict established truths. This is similar to those who claim that Allah only acts for a purpose; they have likened Allah to a human being, arriving at a conclusion that implies Allah, Exalted is He, is in need of a purpose. Such statements apply to humans, not to Allah. If the logic of these individuals were applied to itself, it could lead to conclusions that contradict their own results and are flawed. For example, one of Allah's actions is creation, and it could be said: Allah existed alone before creating anything. He either created for a purpose or without a purpose. If He created for a purpose, that purpose must have existed eternally, yet He did not create them from eternity, leaving only the possibility that He created without a purpose, which is flawed. The source of this flaw lies in the application of reason to matters beyond its knowledge, similar to those who assert a purpose. Furthermore, the error itself permeates the conclusions they have built upon. They have stated: Allah does not act except for a benefit, and the benefit must either pertain to Allah, Exalted is He, or to the servant. Allah is free from harm and benefit, so it must pertain to the servant. This also delves into the unknown. By employing their own logic, one could argue: Allah does not act except for a benefit, and the benefit must either pertain to Allah or to the servant. Since His action, Exalted is He, is the creation of the servant, the benefit of this creation must either pertain to Allah, Exalted is He, or to the servant. It cannot pertain to the servant because prior to creation, he is nonexistent, and no benefit can be conceived for the nonexistent.
