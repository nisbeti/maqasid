In the eighth issue of the first type of the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia), he says: "The benefits that are legislated and the harms that are repelled by law are only considered in terms of how they establish worldly life for the sake of the Hereafter, not in terms of the whims of souls in seeking their ordinary benefits or averting their ordinary harms" (1).

These two texts from al-Shatibi in the fifth and eighth issues were seen by Dr. Darraz as contradictory; therefore, he commented on the first text by saying: "The limitation of this view will come in the eighth issue" (2). He commented on the second text by saying: "It is necessary to limit the fifth issue by this so that it does not contradict the apparent meaning there... He based the benefit and harm on what predominates among them in terms of establishing life and attaining the desires that are necessitated by the sensual qualities of man, making it something that is based on being a sought benefit or a repelled harm. Here he says that mere consideration of it as a benefit in the eyes of a person is not considered; what is considered is that it should be such that worldly life is established for the Hereafter, which can only be the case in accordance with the framework of the law that knows the benefit from this perspective, reconciling it with what he has established in the reality of existence" (3). He also said, commenting on another text from al-Shatibi on the same topic: "This alone does not benefit after having previously considered that what predominates in terms of benefit is the benefit, and what is predominant in terms of harm is the harm... Therefore, we said that it is necessary for the correctness of the statement to limit what has preceded by this" (4).
