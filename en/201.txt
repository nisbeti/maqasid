However, al-Shatibi does not determine whether the status of pardon is a ruling or not. He says: "However, the consideration remains regarding whether pardon is a ruling or not, and if it is said to be a ruling, does it pertain to the address of obligation (taklif) or to the address of status (wada)? All of this is possible, but since it does not lead to a practical ruling, clarification in this matter is not emphasized, and it is preferable to leave it. And Allah is the Guide to the Truth" (1).
Licenses and Determination:
What applies to licenses, according to al-Shatibi's methodology, also applies to what is permissible (mubah). He says: "The ruling of a license is permissibility absolutely, in terms of it being a license" (2). He also states: "Is the permissibility attributed to the license of the kind that removes hardship or of the kind that allows choice between action and abstention? What appears from the texts of licenses is that it is in the sense of removing hardship, not in the other sense" (3).
